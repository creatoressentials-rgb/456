<h2>YouTube Branding Watermark</h2>

<h2>What is a YouTube Branding Watermark?</h2>

<p>A <a href="https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525">YouTube Branding Watermark</a> is a semi-transparent image or logo added by creators to their videos, usually positioned in the bottom right corner. It serves dual purposes: branding and protection.</p>

<p>As a branding tool, it reinforces the creator's brand identity across all their videos. As a protective measure, it deters unauthorized use or distribution of the video content. The watermark also has interactive elements; when viewers hover over or click on it, they can directly <a href="/glossary/youtube-subscribers/">subscribe</a> to the channel.</p>

<h2>How do you add a brand watermark on YouTube?</h2>

<p>To add a brand watermark to your YouTube videos, follow these steps:</p>

<ol>
<li>
<p>Log in to your YouTube account and navigate to YouTube Studio.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>From the left-hand menu, select "Customization" and then navigate to the "Branding" tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>In the "Video watermark" section, click on the "Change" or "Upload" button to add your watermark image.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose the file from your computer (ensuring it meets YouTube’s specifications for watermarks).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>After uploading, you can adjust the display timing of the watermark (entire video, end of video, custom start time).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click “Publish” to save changes. The watermark will now appear on all your uploaded videos at your specified time.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>What size is the YouTube channel Branding watermark?</h2>

<p>The recommended size for a YouTube channel branding watermark is 150 x 150 pixels. </p>

<p>This size is optimal as it ensures clarity and visibility across different devices and screen resolutions. The image should be uploaded in PNG format with a transparent background to integrate seamlessly with the video content without obstructing crucial parts.</p>

<h2>What are some YouTube Branding Watermark best practices?</h2>

<p>Here are some best practices for creating and using a YouTube Branding Watermark:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>Visibility and Clarity: Ensure your watermark is clear and visible without overpowering the video content. A semi-transparent logo can maintain visibility while not being overly intrusive.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Brand Consistency: Use an image that is representative of your brand, such as a logo or brand symbol. This reinforces brand recognition and helps with viewer recall.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Positioning and Size: While YouTube places the watermark in the bottom right corner by default, ensure your design fits well within this space and does not block any essential content in your videos.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Interactivity and Purpose: Remember that the watermark serves as a subscription button when hovered over or clicked. Design it in a way that encourages viewers to subscribe, such as incorporating a call-to-action like "Subscribe" or using a recognizable subscription icon.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Testing and Optimization: Monitor the performance of your watermark in terms of viewer engagement and subscription rates. Experiment with different designs or calls-to-action to find what works best for your audience.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Compliance with YouTube Policies: Ensure your watermark complies with YouTube's community and copyright guidelines to avoid any potential issues with your content.</p>
</li>
</ul>
