<h2>Finalized Earnings</h2>
<p><strong>Finalized earnings</strong> are the previous month's YouTube earnings after Google's monthly adjustment and finalization process. They are posted to the creator's AdSense for YouTube payments account and contribute to the payable balance.</p>
<p>YouTube says finalized earnings are visible only in AdSense for YouTube, not as a final payment ledger inside YouTube Analytics.</p>

<h3>When Are YouTube Earnings Finalized?</h3>
<p>Finalized YouTube earnings for the previous month are generally added to the AdSense for YouTube account balance between the <strong>7th and 12th</strong> of the following month.</p>
<p>Example:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Revenue is generated during June.</li>
  <li>YouTube Analytics shows changing estimates during June and early July.</li>
  <li>The June earnings are finalized and posted between July 7 and July 12.</li>
  <li>If payment conditions are met, Google generally issues the payment between July 21 and July 26.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Where Can You See Finalized Earnings?</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Sign in to AdSense for YouTube.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Payments</strong>.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Payments info</strong> or the YouTube payments account.</li>
  <li>Review the current balance and transaction history.</li>
  <li>Open transaction details for earnings, adjustments, withholding, and payments.</li>
</ol>
<p>Creators with one channel connected to the payments account may also see payment information in the YouTube Studio mobile app, but AdSense for YouTube remains the authoritative finalized-earnings location.</p>

<h3>What Adjustments Happen Before Finalization?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Invalid traffic deductions</li>
  <li><a href="/glossary/content-id/">Content ID</a> claim allocation</li>
  <li>Copyright-dispute outcomes</li>
  <li>Certain ad-campaign reconciliations</li>
  <li>Refunds and chargebacks</li>
  <li>Rights-clearance or revenue-sharing adjustments</li>
  <li>Applicable U.S. tax withholding</li>
  <li>Other policy or payment-account adjustments</li>
</ul>
<p>Not every deduction appears as a separate visible line item. Some invalid-activity deductions can be incorporated during finalization.</p>

<h3>Are Finalized Earnings the Same as Payment?</h3>
<p>No. Finalization and payment issuance are separate stages.</p>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Stage</th><th>Typical Timing</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Revenue generation</td><td>During the month</td><td>The channel accrues estimated earnings</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Finalization</td><td>7th–12th of the following month</td><td>Previous month's YouTube earnings post to AdSense for YouTube</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Threshold and hold check</td><td>By the 20th</td><td>The balance and account must satisfy payment conditions</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Payment issued</td><td>Usually 21st–26th</td><td>Google sends an eligible payment to the selected method</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Payment received</td><td>Depends on method and institution</td><td>The bank, wire service, check delivery, or provider completes receipt</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>What Must Happen Before Payment?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>The account must reach the currency-specific payment threshold.</li>
  <li>Identity verification must be completed when required.</li>
  <li>Address verification must be completed when required.</li>
  <li>Tax information must be submitted.</li>
  <li>A valid payment method must be selected.</li>
  <li>No payment hold can block issuance.</li>
  <li>The creator must remain compliant with AdSense and YouTube payment policies.</li>
</ul>
<p>For U.S. dollar payments, Google's current AdSense threshold table lists a $100 payment threshold. Other currencies have different thresholds.</p>

<h3>What Happens Below the Payment Threshold?</h3>
<p>Finalized earnings remain in the AdSense for YouTube balance and roll over to the next month. The creator does not lose the money merely because the threshold was not met.</p>
<p>The balance continues accumulating until it reaches the payment threshold, provided the account remains eligible and no other adjustment applies.</p>

<h3>Why Do Finalized Earnings Differ From Studio?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>YouTube Analytics showed an earlier estimate.</li>
  <li>The selected date range does not match one finalized calendar month.</li>
  <li>Invalid traffic was removed.</li>
  <li>A claim or dispute changed revenue ownership.</li>
  <li>Tax withholding reduced the posted amount.</li>
  <li>Refunds or transaction adjustments occurred.</li>
  <li>Studio and AdSense use different reporting currencies or presentation.</li>
  <li>The creator is comparing revenue with balance or payment rather than the finalized monthly line item.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Finalized Earnings vs. Balance</h3>
<p>Finalized earnings are monthly earnings entries. Balance is the total payments-account amount after combining finalized earnings, prior rollover, credits, debits, withholding, issued payments, and other transactions.</p>
<p>A $500 finalized monthly earning entry does not guarantee a $500 current balance if the account already received a payment or incurred an adjustment.</p>

<h3>Finalized Earnings vs. Payment Pending</h3>
<p>Payment pending means Google has begun issuing the eligible balance to the payment method. Finalized earnings can sit in the account for days or months without becoming payment pending when the threshold, hold, or verification conditions are not met.</p>
<p>Do not report payment as received merely because earnings finalized.</p>

<h3>Can Finalized Earnings Change Later?</h3>
<p>"Finalized" means the monthly estimate has completed the ordinary finalization process. The payments account can still receive later debits, credits, chargebacks, tax adjustments, invalid-activity deductions, or policy-related changes where allowed.</p>
<p>For accounting, reconcile the finalized earnings entry, the account's later transaction activity, and the amount actually deposited.</p>

<h3>Are Finalized Earnings Taxable?</h3>
<p>Tax treatment depends on the creator's country, entity, accounting method, and applicable law. Google requires tax information from YouTube creators and may withhold U.S. tax on eligible U.S.-viewer earnings.</p>
<p>YouTube and Google state that they do not provide individual tax advice. Creators should use finalized payment records and consult an appropriate tax professional.</p>

<h3>Where Is Tax Withholding Shown?</h3>
<p>YouTube says applicable tax withholding is visible in AdSense for YouTube rather than the YouTube Analytics estimate. Payment and transaction pages can show deductions related to withholding or fees.</p>
<p>The finalized earnings figure, payments-account balance, and bank deposit may therefore differ depending on how the interface presents withholding and issued payments.</p>

<h3>How to Reconcile Monthly YouTube Income</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Export or record the month's YouTube Analytics estimate for performance analysis.</li>
  <li>Wait for finalization between the 7th and 12th.</li>
  <li>Record the finalized YouTube earnings transaction in AdSense.</li>
  <li>Review withholding, debits, credits, and prior balance.</li>
  <li>Match the payment-pending entry to the amount issued.</li>
  <li>Match the bank deposit to the payment receipt.</li>
  <li>Retain receipts, statements, and tax documents.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Why Have Earnings Not Finalized Yet?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>It is before the 7th–12th finalization window.</li>
  <li>The creator is viewing the wrong payments account.</li>
  <li>The channel was linked to a different AdSense for YouTube account.</li>
  <li>Payment or monetization review is ongoing.</li>
  <li>A policy, termination, or account issue affected the earnings.</li>
  <li>The creator is expecting current-month earnings to finalize before the month ends.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use the correct YouTube payments account and compare the exact earning month.</p>

<h3>Common Finalized-Earnings Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Calling the Analytics estimate finalized.</li>
  <li>Assuming finalization means payment has been sent.</li>
  <li>Comparing a custom Studio date range with one calendar-month AdSense entry.</li>
  <li>Ignoring tax withholding and account adjustments.</li>
  <li>Expecting payment below the threshold.</li>
  <li>Looking at the ordinary AdSense payments account instead of the YouTube payments account.</li>
  <li>Treating balance and monthly earnings as identical.</li>
  <li>Recording the bank deposit without reconciling the finalized transaction.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>When are YouTube earnings finalized?</h4>
<p>YouTube says the previous month's earnings are generally posted between the 7th and 12th of the following month.</p>

<h4>Where can I see finalized earnings?</h4>
<p>They are visible in AdSense for YouTube.</p>

<h4>Does finalized mean paid?</h4>
<p>No. Payment is generally issued later, usually between the 21st and 26th when all payment conditions are met.</p>

<h4>Why is the final amount lower than Analytics?</h4>
<p>Adjustments can include invalid traffic, claims, disputes, taxes, refunds, and campaign reconciliation.</p>
