<h2>Production Company</h2>
<p>A <strong>production company</strong> is a business that develops, organizes, creates, and delivers media.</p>
<p>It can produce:</p>
<ul>
<li>YouTube videos</li>
<li>Commercials</li>
<li>Sponsored content</li>
<li>Films</li>
<li>Television</li>
<li>Podcasts</li>
<li>Livestreams</li>
<li>Photography</li>
<li>Animation</li>
<li>Social ads</li>
<li>Courses</li>
<li>Branded series</li>
<li>Corporate video</li>
</ul>
<p>A production company may manage the entire project or only selected stages.</p>

<h3>Production company vs. creator</h3>
<p>A creator can hire a production company while retaining:</p>
<ul>
<li>Channel ownership</li>
<li>Creative approval</li>
<li>Copyright</li>
<li>Publishing control</li>
<li>Audience relationship</li>
</ul>
<p>Alternatively, the production company can own and operate the project.</p>
<p>The contract should identify who is the producer, client, owner, publisher, and distributor.</p>

<h3>Ownership and work made for hire</h3>
<p>Paying a production company does not automatically answer copyright ownership.</p>
<p>The agreement should address:</p>
<ul>
<li>Final video</li>
<li>Raw footage</li>
<li>Project files</li>
<li>Scripts</li>
<li>Graphics</li>
<li>Music</li>
<li>Templates</li>
<li>Rejected concepts</li>
<li>Preexisting materials</li>
<li>Talent releases</li>
</ul>
<p>A qualifying <a href="/glossary/work-made-for-hire">work made for hire</a>, copyright assignment, or license can produce different ownership results.</p>
<h3>Raw files</h3>
<p>Raw-file delivery should specify:</p>
<ul>

<p>Possessing a file does not automatically transfer copyright.</p>
<h3>Production company in creator campaigns</h3>
<p>A brand or creator can hire a production company to make:</p>
<ul>
<li>UGC-style ad assets</li>
<li>Dedicated sponsored video</li>
<li>Product photography</li>
<li>Partnership ads</li>
<li>Brand-account content</li>
<li>Creator-owned series</li>
<li>Livestream</li>
<li>Short-form package</li>
</ul>
<p>The production company may never publish the content itself.</p>

<h3>Frequently asked questions</h3>
<h4>Does a production company own the video?</h4>
<p>Not automatically. Ownership depends on copyright law and the written agreement.</p>
<h4>Is a production company the same as an agency?</h4>
<p>No. Production companies primarily make media. Agencies commonly provide strategy, campaign, or representation services.</p>
<h4>Do production companies provide equipment?</h4>
<p>Many do, directly or through rentals, but the quote should identify equipment and related costs.</p>
<h4>Should raw files be included?</h4>
<p>Only when the scope says so. Raw footage and project files are separate deliverables with storage, transfer, and rights implications.</p>
