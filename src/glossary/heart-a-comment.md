<h2>Heart a Comment</h2>
<p>To <strong>heart a comment</strong> on YouTube means the channel creator has used the heart reaction to show appreciation for a viewer's comment.</p>
<p>The viewer sees the creator's channel picture with a small red heart on the comment. Depending on the viewer's notification settings, YouTube may also notify them that the channel owner loved their comment.</p>

<h3>Where Can a Creator Heart Comments?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Comments under the creator's videos</li>
  <li>Comments under eligible Shorts</li>
  <li>Comments on the creator's YouTube posts</li>
  <li>The Published comments area in YouTube Studio</li>
</ul>

<h3>How to Heart a Comment</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open the video's comments or YouTube Studio's Community area.</li>
  <li>Find a published comment.</li>
  <li>Select the heart icon beneath or beside the comment.</li>
</ol>
<p>The creator can remove the heart by selecting the active heart again where the interface allows.</p>

<h3>What Does the Viewer See?</h3>
<p>A hearted comment displays:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The creator's channel avatar</li>
  <li>A small red heart</li>
  <li>The ordinary comment text and engagement controls</li>
</ul>
<p>The heart publicly signals that the channel appreciated or acknowledged the comment.</p>

<h3>Does the Viewer Receive a Notification?</h3>
<p>They may. YouTube says viewers can receive a notification that a channel owner loved their comment, depending on their notification settings.</p>
<p>Creators should not assume every heart generates a delivered push or email notification.</p>

<h3>Heart vs. Like</h3>

<h3>Heart vs. Pinned Comment</h3>
<p>A heart recognizes the comment without moving it to the top. A pinned comment receives the designated top position for that video or post.</p>
<p>A creator can both heart and pin the same comment when it deserves recognition and broad visibility.</p>

<h3>Heart vs. Reply</h3>
<p>A reply communicates a specific response. A heart provides quick appreciation without adding another written message.</p>
<p>Use a reply when the comment asks a question, needs clarification, or raises an important issue. Use a heart when acknowledgment is enough.</p>

<h3>Does a Heart Mean the Creator Agrees?</h3>
<p>Not necessarily. A creator may heart a comment because it is thoughtful, funny, supportive, or useful.</p>
<p>However, viewers may interpret the heart as approval. Avoid hearting comments containing:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Incorrect factual claims</li>
  <li>Harassment</li>
  <li>Dangerous advice</li>
  <li>Undisclosed promotion</li>
  <li>Misleading summaries</li>
  <li>Statements the channel does not want to endorse</li>
</ul>

<h3>Suggested Comments to Heart</h3>
<p>YouTube Studio can suggest recent comments that its systems identify as having positive intent and being potentially "heart-worthy."</p>
<p>The feature is currently available only in the YouTube Studio mobile app.</p>

<h3>How Suggested Hearts Work</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open the YouTube Studio mobile app.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Community</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Comments to ❤️</strong>.</li>
  <li>Review the suggested comments.</li>
  <li>Heart individual comments or choose to add hearts to all reviewed suggestions.</li>
</ol>
<p>If the section is missing, YouTube says the channel may not have enough comments to generate suggestions.</p>

<h3>Are Suggested Hearts Automatic?</h3>
<p>No. YouTube supplies suggestions, but the creator reviews and confirms the action.</p>
<p>Creators should read every suggested comment before bulk-hearting because automated intent detection can misunderstand sarcasm, context, or a factually incorrect statement.</p>

<h3>What Does Clear Suggestion Do?</h3>
<p>Clearing a suggested heart removes the item from the suggestion list. It does not delete the comment from the channel.</p>
<p>This distinction matters because suggestion management is not comment moderation.</p>

<h3>How Suggestions Are Chosen</h3>
<p>YouTube describes the suggestions as recent comments identified to have positive intent. It does not publish a complete formula, ranking threshold, or guaranteed definition of "heart-worthy."</p>
<p>The system can miss valuable comments or suggest comments the creator does not want to endorse.</p>

<h3>Can Hearts Help Identify Engaged Viewers?</h3>
<p>YouTube Studio's Community spotlight uses several signals to identify engaged audience members, and YouTube lists receiving a high number of creator hearts as one possible signal.</p>
<p>This does not mean hearts create a formal loyalty score or guarantee that someone appears in Community spotlight.</p>

<h3>Do Hearts Affect Comment Ranking?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not publish a guarantee that a creator heart moves a comment to the top or directly controls comment ranking.</p>
<p>A heart can attract attention and influence human engagement, but pinning is the tool that manually places one comment in the top position.</p>

<h3>Do Hearts Affect Video Recommendations?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not describe creator hearts as a direct video-recommendation ranking factor.</p>
<p>Thoughtful interaction may strengthen community participation, but creators should not heart indiscriminately in an attempt to manipulate reach.</p>

<h3>Using Hearts Strategically</h3>
<p>Useful comments to heart include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Thoughtful viewer experiences</li>
  <li>Helpful answers to another viewer</li>
  <li>Constructive feedback</li>
  <li>Accurate additional context</li>
  <li>Long-term supporters</li>
  <li>Funny comments that fit the channel tone</li>
  <li>Specific success stories</li>
</ul>

<h3>Hearts and Critical Comments</h3>
<p>A creator can heart respectful criticism to show that feedback was heard. This can demonstrate openness without requiring agreement with every point.</p>
<p>When the issue needs explanation, add a reply rather than relying on a heart alone.</p>

<h3>Hearts and Questions</h3>
<p>Hearting a question without answering it can confuse viewers. Use the heart to acknowledge the question, then reply when the answer is available.</p>
<p>For recurring questions, consider:</p>
<ul>
  <li>A pinned answer</li>
  <li>An updated description</li>
  <li>A follow-up video</li>
  <li>A frequently asked questions resource</li>
</ul>

<h3>Hearts on Post Comments</h3>
<p>Creators can heart comments on posts. Viewers see the channel picture and red heart, and may receive a notification depending on settings.</p>
<p>This can help reward poll explanations, community stories, and useful discussion under non-video content.</p>

<h3>Can a Moderator Heart Comments?</h3>
<p>YouTube describes hearts as an action from the channel owner and displays the channel's identity. Comment-moderator permission documentation focuses on holding, reporting, hiding, and user management rather than assigning the creator-heart action.</p>
<p>Delegated channel permissions may expose different engagement tools, so the channel should test the exact role rather than assume ordinary comment-moderator access includes hearts.</p>

<h3>Hearting at Scale</h3>
<p>For channels with many comments:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Use filters to find questions, unresponded comments, or member comments.</li>
  <li>Review suggested hearts in the mobile Studio app.</li>
  <li>Set a regular comment-engagement window.</li>
  <li>Do not bulk-heart without reading.</li>
  <li>Prioritize comments that improve the conversation.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Heart Removal</h3>
<p>Removing a heart removes the visible creator-heart signal. YouTube does not promise that a previously delivered notification will be withdrawn from the viewer's device or history.</p>
<p>Use care before hearting sensitive or controversial comments.</p>

<h3>Hearting and Moderation</h3>
<p>A heart does not protect a comment from later removal, reporting, blocked-word review, policy enforcement, or a hidden-user action.</p>
<p>If a comment violates channel rules, moderate it rather than using a heart to signal engagement.</p>

<h3>Common Heart-a-Comment Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Confusing a heart with a like.</li>
  <li>Assuming a heart pins the comment.</li>
  <li>Bulk-hearting suggestions without review.</li>
  <li>Hearting misinformation that appears supportive.</li>
  <li>Using a heart instead of answering a real question.</li>
  <li>Assuming every viewer receives a notification.</li>
  <li>Believing hearts directly boost video recommendations.</li>
  <li>Clearing a suggestion and thinking the comment was deleted.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>What happens when a creator hearts my comment?</h4>
<p>The comment displays the creator's channel picture with a red heart, and you may receive a notification depending on your settings.</p>

<h4>Is a heart the same as a like?</h4>
<p>No. A heart is creator recognition; a like is a general thumbs-up reaction.</p>

<h4>Does a heart move the comment to the top?</h4>
<p>No. Pinning controls the designated top position.</p>

<h4>Does YouTube automatically heart suggested comments?</h4>
<p>No. The creator reviews and confirms suggested hearts in the Studio mobile app.</p>
