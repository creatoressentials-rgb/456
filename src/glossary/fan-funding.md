<h2>Fan Funding</h2>
<p><strong>Fan funding</strong> is YouTube's umbrella term for the direct-payment tools that let viewers send creators money outside of ad revenue: <strong>Channel Memberships, <a href="/glossary/super-chat/">Super Chat</a>, <a href="/glossary/super-stickers/">Super Stickers</a>,</strong> and <strong><a href="/glossary/super-thanks/">Super Thanks</a></strong>. All four sit behind the same entry-level eligibility bar inside the <a href="/glossary/youtube-partner-program/">YouTube Partner Program</a> (YPP), and all four pay creators the same 70% cut of what fans spend.</p>
<p>If you've hit 500 subscribers and are wondering what you can actually monetize before you qualify for ads, this is it.</p>

<h3>The shared eligibility bar</h3>
<p>You don't apply for each fan-funding tool separately. Hit this bar once, and Channel Memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks all become available (assuming your channel and individual videos meet each tool's own content rules — more below):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>500 subscribers</strong></li>
<li><strong>3 valid public uploads</strong> in the last 90 days</li>
<li><strong>3,000 valid public watch hours</strong> in the last 12 months, or <strong>3 million valid public Shorts views</strong> in the last 90 days</li>
<li>No active Community Guidelines strikes</li>
<li>2-Step Verification turned on for your Google Account</li>
<li>An active AdSense for YouTube account linked to your channel</li>
<li>Channel based in an eligible country/region and compliant with YouTube's monetization policies</li>
</ul>
<p>This is the lower of YouTube's two YPP tiers. It does <strong>not</strong> unlock <a href="/glossary/ad-revenue/">ad revenue</a> or YouTube Premium <a href="/glossary/revenue-share/">revenue share</a> — that requires the higher tier (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days). Reaching fan-funding eligibility is a real milestone, but don't mistake it for full monetization.</p>

<h3>The four fan-funding tools</h3>
<p><strong>Channel Memberships</strong> — Recurring monthly payments in exchange for members-only perks: badges, emoji, member-only posts, and more. Up to 5 price tiers between $0.99 and $49.99. This is the only fan-funding tool that's recurring rather than one-off. It has its own detailed eligibility, setup, and tier-pricing rules — see the full <a href="/glossary/channel-memberships/">Channel Memberships</a> entry for the complete breakdown.</p>
<p><strong>Super Chat</strong> — A paid message that gets highlighted and pinned in live chat during live streams and Premieres. Viewers pay $1–$500; the more they spend, the longer the message stays pinned (from a few seconds up to 5 hours) and the more attention-grabbing its color. Not available on age-restricted, unlisted, private, or <a href="/glossary/made-for-kids/">Made for Kids</a> streams, or when live chat is turned off. Only works live — there's no Super Chat on regular uploads or Shorts.</p>
<p><strong>Super Stickers</strong> — The animated-image version of Super Chat: fans buy a themed sticker instead of typing a message, and it surfaces in the live chat feed. Same live-stream/Premiere-only availability and policy rules as Super Chat. Pricing works similarly to Super Chat's tiers, though YouTube doesn't publish a single official ceiling the way it does for Super Chat's $500 max.</p>
<p><strong>Super Thanks</strong> — A one-time paid animation plus a highlighted, customizable comment — available on published long-form videos and Shorts, 24/7, no live stream required. This is the only Super that works on Shorts, and it's the only one available to creators who never go live. It becomes available on a stream's replay once the stream ends, but not while it's live. Not available on <a href="/glossary/content-id/">Content ID</a>-claimed, age-restricted, unlisted, private, Made for Kids, or comments-off videos. Sold at a handful of fixed price points (commonly cited as $2 / $5 / $10 / $50, varying somewhat by region and currency).</p>

<h3>The revenue split</h3>
<p>Every fan-funding tool pays creators <strong>70%</strong>, with YouTube keeping <strong>30%</strong> — the same split whether it's a membership, a Super Chat, a sticker, or a Super Thanks. The 70% is calculated after local sales tax and iOS App Store fees; YouTube covers card-processing costs itself. Earnings route through your linked AdSense account alongside your other YouTube income.</p>
<p>One important legal note: YouTube is explicit that Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks are <strong>not</strong> crowdfunding or donation tools. How that money gets taxed or treated depends on your local laws — worth a conversation with an accountant once fan funding becomes a real income line.</p>

<h3>Which tool fits your content</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Live streamers (gaming, Q&amp;A, IRL):</strong> Super Chat and Super Stickers happen in real time during the broadcast; pair them with Super Thanks on the archived replay afterward.</li>
<li><strong>Long-form-only creators who don't stream live:</strong> Super Thanks and Channel Memberships are your two options — Super Chat and Super Stickers require live chat, so they're off the table.</li>
<li><strong>Shorts creators:</strong> Super Thanks is your only Super — Super Chat and Super Stickers don't apply outside live streams. Channel Memberships work fine, and Shorts views even count toward the eligibility bar itself.</li>
<li><strong>Anyone wanting predictable, recurring income:</strong> Channel Memberships beat one-off Supers for building a forecastable revenue base, though most established creators run memberships and Supers side by side rather than choosing one.</li>
</ul>

<h3>FAQ</h3>
<h4>Do I need 1,000 subscribers to use fan funding?</h4>
<p>No. Fan funding — Channel Memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks — unlocks at 500 subscribers (plus the other expanded-YPP requirements). The 1,000-subscriber threshold is for the separate ad-revenue tier, not fan funding. Some older guides still cite 1,000 subscribers for Super Chat specifically; that's outdated.</p>
<h4>Can I get Super Chats on Shorts?</h4>
<p>No. Super Chat and Super Stickers only work during live streams and Premieres. For Shorts, use Super Thanks instead — it's the only Super that works on non-live content.</p>
<h4>Is fan funding the same as a donation or tip jar?</h4>
<p>Not legally. YouTube explicitly classifies Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks as paid features, not crowdfunding or donation tools, which can affect how the income is taxed depending on where you're based.</p>
