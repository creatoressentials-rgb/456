<h2>Unlisted Video</h2>
<p>An <strong>Unlisted video</strong> is a YouTube video that does not appear as an ordinary Public upload on the creator's channel or in YouTube search, but can be watched by anyone who has the link.</p>
<p>Viewers do not need a Google Account to watch an Unlisted video. Anyone with the URL can also reshare it, which means Unlisted is hidden from ordinary discovery—not confidential or access-controlled.</p>

<h3>Who Can Watch an Unlisted Video?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>The creator</li>
  <li>Anyone who receives the link directly</li>
  <li>Anyone the original recipient forwards the link to</li>
  <li>Visitors to a website or app where the video is embedded</li>
  <li>People who encounter it through a Public playlist that includes it</li>
</ul>
<p>There is no invitation list controlling ordinary Unlisted access. Possession of the link is effectively the access mechanism.</p>

<h3>Is Unlisted Secure?</h3>
<p>Unlisted is not a secure video-hosting or digital-rights-management system. The link can be:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Forwarded</li>
  <li>Posted publicly</li>
  <li>Included in a shared document</li>
  <li>Exposed through browser history or analytics</li>
  <li>Copied from an embed</li>
  <li>Added to a Public playlist</li>
  <li>Captured through screen recording</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not use Unlisted for material that would cause serious harm if it spread, such as client records, tax documents, private medical information, passwords, internal investigations, or unreleased confidential business data.</p>

<h3>Can Viewers Comment?</h3>
<p>Yes. YouTube's visibility comparison says Unlisted videos can be commented on. The creator can still moderate, hold, limit, or disable comments using the video's comment settings.</p>
<p>Comments are one reason creators may choose Unlisted instead of Private for client reviews, classroom discussions, or feedback sessions.</p>

<h3>Can an Unlisted Video Be Embedded?</h3>
<p>Yes, when embedding is allowed. Unlisted videos are commonly embedded in:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Course websites</li>
  <li>Client portals</li>
  <li>Internal documentation</li>
  <li>Portfolio pages</li>
  <li>Event pages</li>
  <li>Help centers</li>
</ul>
<p>The embed does not prevent the link or video ID from being discovered and shared. Use a platform with stronger authentication when access must remain limited.</p>

<h3>Can Unlisted Videos Appear in Search?</h3>
<p>YouTube says they do not normally show in search unless someone adds the Unlisted video to a Public playlist. The video can then become visible through that public context.</p>
<p>This is an important exception to the phrase "not searchable." Unlisted reduces ordinary discovery but does not guarantee that the link or video will never become publicly discoverable.</p>

<h3>Do Unlisted Videos Notify Subscribers?</h3>
<p>No. YouTube's visibility table says Unlisted videos do not appear in the subscriber feed. They also do not function like normal Public uploads that can generate upload notifications.</p>
<p>If an existing Unlisted video is later made Public for the first time, it can be published to subscriber surfaces under YouTube's current settings and notification limits.</p>

<h3>Useful Reasons to Choose Unlisted</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Send a client or collaborator a review copy.</li>
  <li>Embed a video on a webpage without listing it on the channel.</li>
  <li>Share an event replay with attendees.</li>
  <li>Provide a portfolio link.</li>
  <li>Test captions, chapters, end screens, and playback before a Public launch.</li>
  <li>Collect comments from a limited intended group.</li>
  <li>Share a classroom or team resource that is not highly confidential.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Unlisted and YouTube Checks</h3>
<p>Unlisted videos can still be reviewed by YouTube systems and human reviewers for <a href="/glossary/ad-suitability-checks/">ad suitability</a>, copyright, and abuse prevention. Hiding a video from search does not exempt it from YouTube policies.</p>
<p>An Unlisted upload can still receive a <a href="/glossary/copyright-claim/">copyright claim</a>, age restriction, removal, or channel-level enforcement.</p>

<h3>Changing an Unlisted Video to Public</h3>
<p>Changing the video to Public normally keeps its existing URL, views, comments, and Analytics history. It becomes available to anyone and can appear on the channel and discovery surfaces.</p>
<p>Before publishing:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Review comments that were added during the Unlisted period.</li>
  <li>Confirm the title and thumbnail are appropriate for a broader audience.</li>
  <li>Finish checks and processing.</li>
  <li>Review chapters, cards, captions, and end screens.</li>
  <li>Confirm subscriber-feed and notification settings.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Changing an Unlisted Video to Private</h3>
<p>When changed to Private, the ordinary Unlisted link stops working for unapproved viewers and existing embeds no longer function for the general audience. Comments become unavailable while the video is Private.</p>
<p>The creator must specifically invite approved accounts to provide Private access.</p>

<h3>Older Unlisted Videos</h3>
<p>YouTube made many Unlisted videos uploaded before January 1, 2017 Private as part of a 2021 security update, unless the creator opted out. Videos uploaded on or after January 1, 2017 were not affected by that specific change.</p>
<p>Creators maintaining old embeds or archived links should check the current visibility and whether the original URL still works.</p>

<h3>Unlisted vs. Members-Only or Paid Access</h3>
<p>Unlisted does not verify payment, membership, employment, course enrollment, or license status. A customer can forward the URL to a non-customer.</p>
<p>Use authenticated course hosting, YouTube Membership tools, or another controlled platform when ongoing eligibility must be checked for each viewer.</p>

<h3>Common Unlisted-Video Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Calling Unlisted Private.</li>
  <li>Sharing highly sensitive information because the video is absent from search.</li>
  <li>Assuming recipients cannot forward the link.</li>
  <li>Adding the video to a Public playlist unintentionally.</li>
  <li>Assuming viewers need a Google Account.</li>
  <li>Expecting subscriber notifications.</li>
  <li>Using Unlisted as paid-access protection.</li>
  <li>Forgetting that YouTube can still review and enforce policies.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>Can anyone watch an Unlisted video?</h4>
<p>Anyone with the link can watch it, subject to any separate age, country, copyright, or platform restriction.</p>

<h4>Can an Unlisted video be shared again?</h4>
<p>Yes. Anyone with the link can reshare it.</p>

<h4>Do viewers need a YouTube account?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says a Google Account is not required for ordinary Unlisted viewing.</p>

<h4>Can Unlisted videos have comments?</h4>
<p>Yes, unless the creator disables or restricts comments through separate settings.</p>
