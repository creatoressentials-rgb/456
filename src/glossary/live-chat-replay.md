<h2>Live Chat Replay</h2>
<p><strong>YouTube Live Chat Replay</strong> is the archived chat experience that plays messages alongside a completed live stream at the times they originally appeared.</p>
<p>YouTube turns chat replay on by default for stream archives when live chat was available and the archive remains eligible.</p>

<h3>How Live Chat Replay Works</h3>
<p>When a viewer watches an archived live stream, the replayed chat advances with the video's timeline. Seeking forward or backward moves the chat to the corresponding point in the broadcast.</p>
<p>The replay helps viewers understand:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Audience reactions</li>
  <li>Questions the creator was answering</li>
  <li>Moderator announcements</li>
  <li>Community jokes and context</li>
  <li>Highlighted fan-funding messages</li>
  <li>Live Q&amp;A activity</li>
</ul>

<h3>Live Chat Replay vs. Comments</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Live Chat Replay</th><th>Comments</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Timing</td><td>Synchronized with the live broadcast timeline</td><td>Posted below the archived video</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Origin</td><td>Messages sent while the event was live</td><td>Messages posted before or after the archive is available</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Display</td><td>Beside the video player</td><td>Below the video</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Availability</td><td>Depends on live chat, replay settings, edits, and restrictions</td><td>Depends on the archive's comment settings</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>After a live stream ends, new messages are posted as comments rather than added to the historical chat timeline.</p>

<h3>Is Replay On by Default?</h3>
<p>Yes. YouTube's current moderation guidance says Live Chat Replay is turned on by default for live-stream archives.</p>
<p>The creator can disable it for an archived stream.</p>

<h3>How to Turn Off Live Chat Replay</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio or the creator's video-management page.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Content</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open the <strong>Live</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Select the archived stream.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Customization</strong>.</li>
  <li>Deselect <strong>Live chat replay</strong>.</li>
  <li>Save the change.</li>
</ol>
<p>Interface wording can change, but the control applies to the archived chat display rather than deleting the video archive itself.</p>

<h3>What Happens If the Stream Is Edited?</h3>
<p>YouTube says a live stream edited in the YouTube video editor will no longer have chat replay. This applies to every type of editor function.</p>
<p>Actions that can remove replay include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Trimming the archive</li>
  <li>Cutting a section</li>
  <li>Blurring a face or object</li>
  <li>Applying another supported Studio Editor change</li>
</ul>
<p>Because Studio Editor changes may also be irreversible, preserve a local archive and consider the chat consequence before editing.</p>

<h3>Why Does Editing Remove Replay?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not publish a detailed technical explanation, but editing changes the relationship between the original live timeline and the archived video.</p>
<p>The practical rule is simple: any current YouTube Video Editor change removes the synchronized chat replay.</p>

<h3>What Is Included in Replay?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Ordinary public live chat messages that appeared during the broadcast</li>
  <li>Creator and moderator messages</li>
  <li>Eligible pinned-message context</li>
  <li>Super Chat and other supported highlighted messages</li>
  <li>Live Q&amp;A sessions that YouTube preserves</li>
</ul>
<p>The replay reflects the moderated public chat experience rather than every attempted message.</p>

<h3>What Is Not Included?</h3>
<p>YouTube specifically says live polls do not appear in Live Chat Replay.</p>
<p>Other missing content can include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Messages held and never approved</li>
  <li>Messages removed by the creator or moderators</li>
  <li>Messages deleted by the viewer</li>
  <li>Content removed for policy or spam reasons</li>
  <li>Private moderation interfaces</li>
  <li>Live reactions as a complete message history</li>
  <li>Poll results that existed only during the live event</li>
</ul>

<h3>Live Q&amp;A and Replay</h3>
<p>YouTube says completed Live Q&amp;A sessions are available in Chat Replay. Unanswered questions are removed from the active question list when the Q&amp;A ends.</p>
<p>This differs from live polls, which are explicitly live-only and omitted from replay.</p>

<h3>Top Chat vs. Live Chat in Replay</h3>
<p>During the live event, viewers can choose:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Top Chat:</strong> A filtered view intended to show messages viewers are likely to value</li>
  <li><strong>Live Chat:</strong> The unfiltered public message stream as messages arrive</li>
</ul>
<p>The replay represents the archived public chat experience. It does not expose messages that moderation systems never made public.</p>

<h3>Can a Viewer Delete an Old Chat Message?</h3>
<p>YouTube lets viewers open their Live Chat history through YouTube History and delete messages they previously posted.</p>
<p>Deleting a paid <a href="/glossary/super-chat/">Super Chat</a> message after the stream does not create a refund.</p>

<h3>Made-for-Kids Streams</h3>
<p>When a live stream is set as <a href="/glossary/made-for-kids/">Made for Kids</a>, YouTube disables live chat and live chat replay.</p>
<p>A creator cannot restore replay for a stream that never had eligible chat because of the audience setting.</p>

<h3>Other Reasons Replay May Be Missing</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Live chat was disabled during the stream.</li>
  <li>The archive is still processing.</li>
  <li>The creator turned replay off.</li>
  <li>The archive was edited in YouTube Studio.</li>
  <li>The stream exceeded archive limits or was not captured correctly.</li>
  <li>Policy or safety restrictions disabled chat.</li>
  <li>The video or archive was deleted or made inaccessible.</li>
  <li>Live chat was not supported for that event.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Stream Length and Archiving</h3>
<p>YouTube says streams shorter than 12 hours can be automatically archived. Streams longer than 12 hours may not be captured at all.</p>
<p>If no playable archive exists, there is no normal synchronized video timeline for chat replay.</p>

<h3>Replay and Public-to-Members-Only Streams</h3>
<p>A public-to-members-only transition creates separate stream records. Each archive and its chat experience should be reviewed separately.</p>
<p>The members-only archive remains restricted to qualifying members, including its associated replay where available.</p>

<h3>Replay and Subscriber-Only Chat</h3>
<p>Messages posted during subscriber-only chat can replay with the archive. The historical messages can continue to show that the participant was eligible to chat during the event.</p>
<p>Creators should remind viewers that live chat can remain public after the broadcast.</p>

<h3>Replay and Slow Mode</h3>
<p>Slow Mode controls how often viewers can submit messages while live. Replay shows the messages at their original times.</p>
<p>The replay does not reproduce each participant's countdown timer or slow down playback.</p>

<h3>Does Replay Affect Analytics?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not provide a single metric showing how many archive viewers opened or followed Live Chat Replay.</p>
<p>Evaluate the archive using:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Views</li>
  <li>Watch time</li>
  <li>Audience retention</li>
  <li>Comments</li>
  <li>Traffic sources</li>
  <li>Concurrent-viewer history</li>
  <li>Live chat message and reaction metrics where available</li>
</ul>

<h3>Benefits of Keeping Replay</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Preserves community context</li>
  <li>Makes Q&amp;A answers easier to understand</li>
  <li>Shows the live-event atmosphere</li>
  <li>Recognizes participating viewers and members</li>
  <li>Provides social proof for future streams</li>
  <li>Helps viewers follow audience reactions</li>
</ul>

<h3>Reasons to Disable Replay</h3>
<ul>
  <li>The chat distracts from an evergreen archive.</li>
  <li>Sensitive information appeared.</li>
  <li>The chat became hostile or irrelevant.</li>
  <li>The creator wants a clean educational replay.</li>
  <li>Moderation quality was poor.</li>
  <li>The archive serves a different audience from the live event.</li>
</ul>
<p>Disabling replay does not remove comments below the archived video.</p>

<h3>How to Preserve Chat Before Editing</h3>
<p>Because any Studio Editor change removes replay, creators who need a record can:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Preserve a local stream recording.</li>
  <li>Capture important questions and timestamps.</li>
  <li>Export available live-chat data through authorized tools where permitted.</li>
  <li>Create a summary or FAQ before editing.</li>
  <li>Keep screenshots of important member or moderator messages.</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not publish private viewer information outside its appropriate context.</p>

<h3>Common Live-Chat-Replay Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Assuming replay is the same as comments.</li>
  <li>Editing the archive without realizing replay will disappear.</li>
  <li>Expecting live polls to appear.</li>
  <li>Assuming every attempted message is preserved.</li>
  <li>Believing disabling replay deletes the video.</li>
  <li>Expecting Slow Mode timers to replay.</li>
  <li>Forgetting that Made-for-Kids streams cannot use replay.</li>
  <li>Relying on YouTube as the only stream archive.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>Is Live Chat Replay turned on automatically?</h4>
<p>Yes. YouTube says replay is on by default for eligible live-stream archives.</p>

<h4>Do live polls appear in replay?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says live polls are visible only during the live event.</p>

<h4>What happens if I trim the archive?</h4>
<p>Any YouTube Video Editor change removes Live Chat Replay.</p>

<h4>Can viewers delete their old chat messages?</h4>
<p>Yes. Viewers can manage and delete their live-chat messages through YouTube History.</p>
