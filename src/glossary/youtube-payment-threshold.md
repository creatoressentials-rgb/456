<h2>YouTube Payment Threshold</h2>
<p>The <strong>YouTube payment threshold</strong> is the minimum finalized balance a creator's AdSense for YouTube payments account must reach before Google can issue a regular payment.</p>
<p>The threshold depends on the reporting currency of the payments account. For accounts reporting in U.S. dollars, Google's current payment threshold is $100.</p>

<h3>What Balance Counts Toward the Threshold?</h3>
<p>The threshold is based on the finalized balance in the creator's AdSense for YouTube payments account—not the current estimated revenue shown in YouTube Analytics.</p>
<p>This distinction matters because:</p>
<ul>
  <li>YouTube Analytics reports provisional revenue during the month.</li>
  <li>Previous-month earnings are generally finalized between the 7th and 12th of the following month.</li>
  <li>Finalized earnings then post to the YouTube payments account.</li>
  <li>The total balance must meet the applicable threshold by the monthly payment cutoff.</li>
</ul>
<p>A creator may see more than $100 in estimated Studio revenue while the AdSense for YouTube balance remains below $100 because the current month's earnings have not finalized yet.</p>

<h3>Payment Threshold vs. Other Revenue Thresholds</h3>
<p>Google uses several different thresholds in the YouTube payment process:</p>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Threshold</th><th>What Happens When It Is Reached?</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Tax information threshold</td><td>The creator must provide required tax information; YPP creators are generally required to submit tax details regardless of location</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Verification threshold</td><td>Identity and address verification become required</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Payment method selection threshold</td><td>The creator can choose an available payment method</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Payment threshold</td><td>The finalized balance becomes eligible for regular payment when all other requirements are satisfied</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Cancellation threshold</td><td>A smaller balance may qualify for a final payment after the account is properly closed</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>Reaching the verification or payment-method threshold does not mean the account has reached the regular payment threshold.</p>

<h3>When Must the Balance Reach the Threshold?</h3>
<p>Google says the total balance must reach the payment threshold by the <strong>20th of the month</strong> to be paid during that month's payment cycle.</p>
<p>If the finalized balance is eligible and the account has no payment holds, Google generally issues payment between the 21st and 26th.</p>
<p>Example:</p>
<ol>
  <li>June earnings finalize between July 7 and July 12.</li>
  <li>The AdSense for YouTube balance reaches $100 before or on July 20.</li>
  <li>Verification, tax information, and payment method are complete.</li>
  <li>No active hold prevents payment.</li>
  <li>Google generally issues payment between July 21 and July 26.</li>
</ol>

<h3>What Happens Below the Threshold?</h3>
<p>The balance rolls over to the next month. It is not forfeited merely because the creator did not reach the threshold during one payment cycle.</p>
<p>For example:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Month 1 finalized balance: $42</li>
  <li>Month 2 additional finalized earnings: $35</li>
  <li>New balance: $77</li>
  <li>Month 3 additional finalized earnings: $31</li>
  <li>New balance: $108</li>
</ul>
<p>If the $108 balance and all payment requirements are in place by the 20th, it can be issued during that month's payment cycle.</p>

<h3>Does Estimated Revenue Count?</h3>
<p>No. <a href="/glossary/estimated-revenue/">Estimated revenue</a> is provisional YouTube Analytics data. The payment threshold is measured against the finalized payments-account balance.</p>
<p>Estimated revenue may change because of:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Invalid traffic</li>
  <li>Copyright claims</li>
  <li>Revenue-sharing adjustments</li>
  <li>Refunds and chargebacks</li>
  <li>Tax withholding</li>
  <li>Campaign reconciliation</li>
</ul>
<p>Only after the monthly finalization process does the amount post to AdSense for YouTube and count toward the balance used for payment.</p>

<h3>Separate AdSense and YouTube Payments Accounts</h3>
<p>If a creator has separate payments accounts for website AdSense and AdSense for YouTube, each account must independently reach its own payment threshold.</p>
<p>For example, a $70 YouTube balance and a $40 website-AdSense balance do not necessarily combine into one $110 payment balance. Each payments account is evaluated separately.</p>
<p>This is one of the most common reasons creators believe they reached the threshold when the YouTube payments account itself remains below it.</p>

<h3>Can You Lower the Payment Threshold?</h3>
<p>Google currently allows creators to raise the payment threshold above the default amount but not lower it below the standard threshold.</p>
<p>A creator may raise the threshold to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Receive fewer, larger payments</li>
  <li>Reduce the number of small bookkeeping entries</li>
  <li>Coordinate payments with business cash-flow preferences</li>
</ul>
<p>Raising the threshold delays payment until the higher balance is reached. Google does not allow creators to change the monthly payment frequency.</p>

<h3>Payment Threshold vs. Payment Method Threshold</h3>
<p>The payment-method selection threshold is lower than the regular payment threshold. It allows the creator to add a bank account or another available payment method before the account is ready for payout.</p>
<p>For a U.S.-dollar YouTube payments account, Google's current table lists:</p>
<ul>
  <li>$10 payment-method selection threshold</li>
  <li>$100 regular payment threshold</li>
</ul>
<p>Adding a bank account at $10 does not mean Google will send the $10 balance.</p>

<h3>Payment Threshold vs. Cancellation Threshold</h3>
<p>The cancellation threshold applies when the creator properly closes the AdSense for YouTube account. For U.S. dollars, Google's current cancellation threshold is $10.</p>
<p>Google says an eligible final payment is generally sent within approximately 90 days after the end of the month when:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The balance exceeds the cancellation threshold</li>
  <li>There are no payment holds</li>
  <li>All required payment steps were completed</li>
</ul>
<p>The lower cancellation threshold is not an early-withdrawal option for an active account.</p>

<h3>Reaching the Threshold Does Not Guarantee Immediate Payment</h3>
<p>The account must also satisfy other requirements:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Completed tax information</li>
  <li>Completed identity verification</li>
  <li>Completed address verification when required</li>
  <li>An active payment method</li>
  <li>No compliance, tax, identity, or payment hold</li>
  <li>Compliance with AdSense and YouTube payment policies</li>
  <li>The threshold reached by the monthly cutoff</li>
</ul>
<p>A creator who fixes a hold after the 20th may need to wait for the next payment cycle.</p>

<h3>Payment Issued vs. Payment Received</h3>
<p>When Google issues a payment, a Payment pending or related transaction can appear in AdSense for YouTube. The money may still need time to reach the creator.</p>
<p>Delivery time varies by:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Electronic Funds Transfer processing</li>
  <li>Wire-transfer processing</li>
  <li>Bank holidays</li>
  <li>The creator's financial institution</li>
  <li>Check delivery</li>
  <li>Country-specific payment providers</li>
</ul>
<p>Reaching the threshold, receiving a Payment pending notice, and seeing the money in the bank are three separate moments.</p>

<h3>Can a Balance Be Withheld After Reaching the Threshold?</h3>
<p>Yes. Google can place holds, investigate policy violations, deduct invalid earnings, or delay payment when account requirements are incomplete.</p>
<p>Review the AdSense for YouTube Payments page and any alerts involving:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Tax</li>
  <li>Identity</li>
  <li>Address PIN</li>
  <li>Payment method</li>
  <li>Compliance</li>
  <li>Returned payments</li>
</ul>

<h3>How to Check Progress Toward the Threshold</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Sign in to AdSense for YouTube.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Payments</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose the YouTube payments account.</li>
  <li>Review the finalized balance.</li>
  <li>Check the threshold progress indicator when available.</li>
  <li>Review holds, verification, tax, and payment-method notices.</li>
</ol>
<p>Do not use the current-month YouTube Studio estimate as the threshold progress number.</p>

<h3>Common Payment-Threshold Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Using estimated Studio revenue instead of the finalized AdSense balance.</li>
  <li>Assuming the threshold is $100 in every currency.</li>
  <li>Combining separate website-AdSense and YouTube balances.</li>
  <li>Confusing payment-method selection with payment eligibility.</li>
  <li>Expecting an immediate transfer the day the threshold is reached.</li>
  <li>Ignoring the 20th-of-the-month cutoff.</li>
  <li>Assuming a payment hold will clear automatically.</li>
  <li>Treating the cancellation threshold as an active-account withdrawal option.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>What is YouTube's payment threshold in the United States?</h4>
<p>For a U.S.-dollar payments account, Google's current regular payment threshold is $100.</p>

<h4>What happens if I earn less than the threshold?</h4>
<p>The finalized balance rolls over until the payments account reaches the required amount.</p>

<h4>Can I combine AdSense and YouTube balances?</h4>
<p>Separate payments accounts must independently reach their thresholds.</p>

<h4>Can I lower the threshold?</h4>
<p>No. Google allows creators to raise the default threshold but not reduce it.</p>
