<h2>YouTube Call-to-Action</h2>

<h2>What is a Call to Action on YouTube?</h2>

<p>A YouTube call to action (CTA) is a prompt that encourages viewers to take a specific action after or during a video. Whether it’s subscribing to your channel, watching another video, visiting your website, or leaving a comment, CTAs are essential for guiding viewers to engage further with your content.</p>

<p>On YouTube, CTAs can be verbal prompts, visual cues, or interactive elements designed to keep viewers engaged, increase channel growth, or drive traffic to other platforms.</p>

<p>For creators, leveraging effective CTAs is a strategic way to boost metrics like watch time, subscriber count, and engagement—all critical factors in YouTube’s algorithm.</p>

<h2>What Are Call to Action YouTube Cards?</h2>

<p>Call to Action YouTube cards are interactive, clickable elements that appear during a video to guide viewers toward taking a specific action. These cards can pop up as annotations in the top corner of a video and are customizable to link viewers to other videos, playlists, or external sites (if the creator is eligible).</p>

<p>YouTube cards are powerful tools for:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>Directing viewers to related content, boosting watch time.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Promoting merchandise or external resources.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Encouraging specific actions, like signing up for a newsletter or exploring a playlist.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>Cards are unobtrusive yet effective, allowing creators to enhance viewer engagement without disrupting the viewing experience.</p>

<h2>How to Do Call to Actions on YouTube Videos (+ Examples)</h2>

<p>Implementing a call to action on YouTube requires creativity and strategy. Here are a few examples and methods to integrate CTAs into your videos:</p>

<p>1. Verbal CTAs:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>At the end of a video: “If you enjoyed this video, don’t forget to like and subscribe for more content like this!”</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>During the video: “Let me know your thoughts in the comments below!”</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>When introducing a playlist: “Check out the playlist linked here for more tips and tricks!”</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>2. Visual CTAs:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>End Screens: Use end screens to prompt viewers to subscribe, watch related videos, or visit a playlist. For example, add a subscribe button and two video suggestions tailored to your audience’s interests.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Banners or Text Overlays: Highlight a CTA like “Hit the like button if you agree!” with an animated overlay during key moments.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>3. Interactive CTAs:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>Cards: Insert a YouTube card suggesting a related video or playlist when discussing a specific topic. For instance, if you’re demonstrating a product, link to another video reviewing the product in-depth.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Pinned Comments: Pin a comment asking viewers to share their thoughts or answer a specific question related to the video.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>4. Creative CTAs:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>Encourage engagement by inviting viewers to vote in a poll or enter a giveaway. For example, “Click the link in the description to vote for next week’s topic!”</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use a short teaser: “Want to know more? Watch my next video for all the details!”</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>5. External Links:</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>Direct viewers to a website: “Visit my site for exclusive content—link in the description!”</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Promote merchandise: “Check out my merch store linked below!”</p>
</li>
</ul>
