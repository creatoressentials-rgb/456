<h2>Affiliate Link</h2>
<p>An <strong>affiliate link</strong> is a trackable URL used to identify traffic or customers referred by a creator, publisher, or other affiliate.</p>
<p>The link normally includes or connects to an affiliate identifier, campaign parameter, promotional-property ID, or other tracking information. If the viewer completes a qualifying action under the program's rules, the system can attribute the action and calculate an <a href="/glossary/affiliate-commission">affiliate commission</a>.</p>
<p>An affiliate link does not guarantee commission, and it does not necessarily change the customer's price.</p>
<h3>What an affiliate link can look like</h3>
<p>An affiliate link may be:</p>
<ul>
<li>A long URL containing tracking parameters</li>
<li>A shortened link supplied by the program</li>
<li>A redirect through an <a href="/glossary/affiliate-network">affiliate network</a></li>
<li>A deep link to a specific product page</li>
<li>A link attached to a button or image</li>
<li>A platform-generated product link</li>
<li>A link in a YouTube description</li>
<li>A link on a blog, newsletter, podcast page, or social profile</li>
<li>A QR code resolving to a tracked URL</li>
</ul>
<p>The visible link can look simple while the tracking system records affiliate, campaign, product, and click information behind the scenes.</p>
<h3>How affiliate links work</h3>
<p>A common process is:</p>
<ol>
<li>The affiliate joins a brand's program.</li>
<li>The program assigns an affiliate or publisher ID.</li>
<li>The creator generates a <a href="/glossary/tracking-link/">tracking link</a>.</li>
<li>The creator adds the link to eligible content.</li>
<li>A viewer clicks the link.</li>
<li>The program records referral information through cookies, URL parameters, server-side data, or another method.</li>
<li>The viewer completes a qualifying action.</li>
<li>The program applies its <a href="/glossary/attribution/">attribution</a> rules.</li>
<li>The transaction remains pending during validation.</li>
<li>An approved commission is paid according to the program's schedule.</li>
</ol>
<p>Tracking methods and program rules vary.</p>

<p>A UTM-tagged link can help with analytics but is not automatically an affiliate link. An affiliate link may also contain <a href="/glossary/utm-parameters/">UTM parameters</a>.</p>
<h3>Deep links</h3>
<p>A <strong>deep link</strong> sends the viewer directly to a specific product, category, subscription page, or other destination rather than the advertiser's homepage.</p>
<p>Deep links can improve the customer experience because the viewer reaches the exact item discussed in the content.</p>
<p>CJ, for example, provides publishers with tools for creating trackable deep links from advertiser websites.</p>
<h3>Cookies and attribution windows</h3>
<p>Many affiliate systems place or read tracking information when the viewer clicks the link.</p>
<p>The <strong>attribution window</strong>—often called a cookie window—is the period during which a qualifying action may be credited.</p>
<p>A 30-day window does not mean every purchase during 30 days belongs to the creator. Credit may be affected by:</p>
<ul>
<li>Another affiliate's later click</li>
<li>Coupon or loyalty attribution</li>
<li>App-to-web movement</li>
<li>Cross-device behavior</li>
<li>Cookie deletion</li>
<li>Consent restrictions</li>
<li>Browser privacy tools</li>
<li>Product exclusions</li>
<li>Customer eligibility</li>
<li>Program-specific priority rules</li>
</ul>
<p>Awin explains that affiliate cookies can store data identifying the advertiser, publisher, click time, and agreed tracking information. Networks may also use other tracking methods.</p>
<h3>Does an affiliate link increase the price?</h3>
<p>Normally, an <a href="/glossary/affiliate-commission/">affiliate commission</a> is paid by the advertiser or program and does not add a separate charge to the customer.</p>
<p>The customer price can still differ because of:</p>
<ul>
<li>Dynamic pricing</li>
<li>Regional pricing</li>
<li>Sales and coupons</li>
<li>Membership status</li>
<li>Taxes and shipping</li>
<li>A creator-specific discount</li>
<li>Product availability</li>
</ul>
<p>Creators should not promise that the link is the lowest available price unless they can support that claim.</p>
<h3>Why an affiliate link might not earn commission</h3>
<p>A creator may receive no commission when:</p>
<ul>
<li>The viewer does not complete a qualifying action</li>
<li>The action happens after the attribution window</li>
<li>Another affiliate receives credit</li>
<li>The product is excluded</li>
<li>The customer returns the order</li>
<li>The transaction is canceled or fraudulent</li>
<li>Tracking is blocked</li>
<li>The purchase occurs on another device</li>
<li>The viewer uses an uncredited coupon</li>
<li>The creator violates the program terms</li>
<li>The program does not track the country or merchant</li>
<li>The commission is below a minimum payment threshold</li>
</ul>
<h3>Affiliate-link disclosure</h3>
<p>An affiliate link creates a financial relationship that viewers may need to understand.</p>
<p>The FTC says <strong>“Paid link”</strong> placed next to an affiliate link can adequately disclose the nature of the link. It has questioned whether <strong>“commissionable link”</strong> clearly communicates that the creator earns money.</p>
<p>Clear wording includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>“I earn a commission if you purchase through this link.”</li>
<li>“This is an affiliate link.”</li>
<li>“Some links are affiliate links, meaning I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”</li>
</ul>
<p>Place the disclosure close to the recommendation and link—not only on a separate disclosure page.</p>
<p>Amazon Associates separately requires participants to identify themselves using its required statement: <strong>“As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”</strong> Amazon also says creators must include a legally compliant disclosure with affiliate links.</p>
<h3>Affiliate links on YouTube</h3>
<p>YouTube describes affiliate links as a way creators can be paid to embed links to brands, products, or services in video descriptions.</p>
<p>Creators should also consider:</p>
<ul>
<li>YouTube's external-links policies</li>
<li>Applicable paid-promotion declarations</li>
<li>Clear spoken or on-screen disclosure when the video endorses the product</li>
<li>Disclosure near links in the description</li>
<li>Product-tag requirements</li>
<li>Program-specific rules</li>
<li>Whether links remain current after products or commission rates change</li>
</ul>
<p>A disclosure in the description may not be enough for an endorsement communicated in the video. FTC guidance recommends putting video disclosures in the video itself.</p>
<h3>Affiliate-link management</h3>
<p>Creators should regularly check:</p>
<ul>
<li>Broken links</li>
<li>Out-of-stock products</li>
<li>Changed destinations</li>
<li>Expired programs</li>
<li>Updated commission rates</li>
<li>Link localization</li>
<li>Disclosure wording</li>
<li>Redirect safety</li>
<li>Program compliance</li>
<li>Analytics and conversion</li>
<li>Links in old evergreen content</li>
</ul>
<p>A link can continue sending traffic after the creator stops earning commission.</p>
<h3>Affiliate-link red flags</h3>
<p>Avoid:</p>
<ul>
<li>Hiding the commercial relationship</li>
<li>Misleading link text</li>
<li>Forced clicks or automatic redirects</li>
<li>Cookie stuffing</li>
<li>Self-referrals prohibited by the program</li>
<li>Publishing private coupon codes</li>
<li>Claiming a discount that does not exist</li>
<li>Shortening links in a way the program forbids</li>
<li>Placing links on prohibited platforms</li>
<li>Making unsupported product claims</li>
<li>Promoting recalled, unsafe, or counterfeit products</li>
<li>Assuming every tracking parameter is harmless</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently asked questions</h3>
<h4>Does clicking an affiliate link cost the viewer money?</h4>
<p>The link normally does not add a separate fee. The creator may earn commission from an eligible purchase or action.</p>
<h4>Does an affiliate link always use a cookie?</h4>
<p>No. Cookies are common, but programs can also use server-side tracking, product tags, account matching, coupon attribution, or other methods.</p>
<h4>How long does an affiliate link track a viewer?</h4>
<p>The attribution window depends on the program and can be changed. Check the current terms rather than assuming a standard duration.</p>
<h4>Do creators have to disclose affiliate links?</h4>
<p>A financial relationship may require clear disclosure. Affiliate programs can also impose their own wording and placement requirements.</p>
