<h2>YouTube Advanced Features</h2>
<p><strong>YouTube Advanced Features</strong> are a channel-level eligibility tier that YouTube unlocks after a creator completes identity verification. Verification requires a phone number plus one additional trust signal — either sufficient channel history, valid ID verification, or video verification. Once unlocked, advanced features include longer daily upload limits, live streaming access, custom <a href="/glossary/youtube-thumbnails/">thumbnails</a>, pinned comments, Content ID claim appeals, and the ability to apply for YouTube Partner Program monetization.</p>
<p>Advanced features sit at the top of a three-tier system: <strong>Standard → Intermediate → Advanced</strong>. Each tier requires more trust from YouTube than the last, and losing that trust (through Community Guidelines strikes or copyright strikes) can push a channel back down a tier.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Not the same as YouTube Advanced Mode.</strong> "Advanced features" (this page) is about what actions your channel is <em>allowed</em> to take. <a href="/glossary/youtube-advanced-mode/">YouTube Advanced Mode</a> is a completely different thing — the expanded analytics/reporting screen inside <a href="/glossary/youtube-studio/">YouTube Studio</a>. The two share a word, nothing else.</p>
</blockquote>

<h3>What Are YouTube Advanced Features?</h3>
<p>Advanced features are the highest tier of a feature-access system YouTube uses to fight spam, scams, and abuse. Rather than granting every tool to every new channel immediately, YouTube phases access in as a channel demonstrates it's run by a real person who follows the rules.</p>
<p>Getting access to advanced features doesn't cost anything and isn't the same as being "YouTube verified" with a checkmark badge, and it isn't the same as YouTube Partner Program monetization — it's a prerequisite building block for some of both, but each has its own separate requirements (see <a href="/glossary/youtube-partner-program/">YouTube Partner Program</a> below).</p>

<h3>How to Verify Your Channel for Advanced Features</h3>
<p>Verification happens in <a href="https://studio.youtube.com">YouTube Studio</a> under <strong>Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility</strong>, and it's a two-step process.</p>

<h4>Step 1: Complete phone verification (unlocks Intermediate)</h4>
<ol>
<li>Sign in to YouTube Studio as the <strong>primary channel owner</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → Intermediate features → Verify phone number</strong>.</li>
<li>Enter a phone number. YouTube sends a code by text or voice call.</li>
<li>Enter the code to confirm.</li>
</ol>
<p>This alone unlocks Intermediate features (longer videos, live streaming, custom thumbnails, podcasts). A phone number can be used to verify a maximum of <strong>2 channels per year</strong>.</p>

<h4>Step 2: Unlock Advanced features</h4>
<p>After phone verification, go to <strong>Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → Advanced features → Access features</strong> and choose one of:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Build channel history.</strong> Keep uploading, stay compliant with YouTube's Community Guidelines, and avoid strikes. Most active, established channels qualify automatically without doing anything extra. YouTube doesn't publish a fixed <a href="/glossary/timeline/">timeline</a> for a first-time unlock, but it states that channels rebuilding <em>lost</em> access can typically do so within about 2 months of clean activity — a reasonable benchmark, not a guarantee.</li>
<li><strong>Verify with a valid ID.</strong> Choose "Use your ID," get an email or scan a QR code, open it on your phone, photograph your ID (the birthdate must match your Google Account), and submit. Review usually takes 24 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Verify with video.</strong> Choose "Use video verification," open the emailed link on your phone, and follow prompts (such as turning your head) to record a short verification video. Review usually takes 24 hours.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> ID and video verification aren't offered to every creator or in every country. If you don't see those options, channel history is your only route.</p>
<p>If a verification attempt is rejected twice, YouTube requires a 30-day wait before trying either method again.</p>

<h3>Can Advanced Features Be Revoked?</h3>
<p>Yes. Advanced feature access isn't permanent. YouTube can restrict it if a channel:</p>
<ul>
<li>Receives a Community Guidelines strike or a <a href="/glossary/copyright-strike/">copyright strike</a></li>
<li>Repeatedly posts spam, scams, or misleading metadata</li>
<li>Engages in harassment, impersonation, or other policy violations</li>
<li>Stops maintaining a positive channel history</li>
</ul>
<p>If access is restricted, YouTube emails the channel owner. Access can be restored the same two ways it was earned: rebuilding channel history, or resubmitting ID or video verification.</p>
<p>ID and video verification data isn't kept indefinitely — it's typically deleted a few months after a channel builds sufficient history, or after 1 year of not using advanced features, with a hard cap of 3 years for abuse-prevention purposes. Creators can also delete it manually anytime from their Google Account, though doing so before establishing channel history means losing advanced-feature access until they rebuild history or re-verify.</p>

<h3>Advanced Features vs. the YouTube Partner Program</h3>
<p>These are related but <strong>not the same thing</strong>. Advanced feature access (channel history or ID/video verification) is one listed component needed to apply for <a href="/glossary/youtube-partner-program/">YouTube Partner Program</a> monetization — but clearing it doesn't make a channel eligible for the Partner Program on its own. Full YPP eligibility separately requires:</p>
<ul>
<li>1,000 subscribers <strong>and</strong> 4,000 valid public watch hours in the trailing 12 months, <strong>or</strong> 1,000 subscribers and 10 million valid public Shorts views in the trailing 90 days</li>
<li>No active Community Guidelines strikes</li>
<li>Two-step verification enabled on the Google Account</li>
<li>Residing in a country where YPP is available</li>
</ul>
<p>In short: advanced features unlock the <em>ability to apply</em>; YPP's own thresholds determine whether that application is <em>accepted</em>.</p>

<h3>Who Can Verify: Owners vs. Delegates</h3>
<p>Only the <strong>primary channel owner</strong> can complete phone, ID, or video verification — this can't be delegated. If your channel uses a Brand Account with multiple people managing it via <a href="/glossary/channel-permissions/">channel permissions</a>, managers, editors, and other roles inherit whatever feature tier the primary owner has verified into. They cannot independently unlock a higher tier for themselves, and they can't complete verification on the owner's behalf.</p>
<p>If you're signed in as a delegate and see the error "Advanced YouTube features aren't available for this account," it almost always means you're not the primary owner — check with whoever holds that role.</p>

<h3>FAQ</h3>
<h4>Is "YouTube Advanced Features" the same as "YouTube Advanced Mode"?</h4>
<p>No. Advanced features is an identity-verification eligibility tier that controls what actions your channel can take (uploads, live streaming, pinned comments, etc.). <a href="/glossary/youtube-advanced-mode/">YouTube Advanced Mode</a> is a Studio analytics reporting view for filtering and exporting performance data. They're unrelated beyond the shared word "advanced."</p>
<h4>Does unlocking advanced features mean my channel is monetized?</h4>
<p>No. It's a prerequisite for <em>applying</em> to the YouTube Partner Program, not a monetization approval. You still need to separately meet YPP's subscriber, watch-time (or Shorts views), and strike-free requirements.</p>
<h4>Can my editor or channel manager verify the channel for me?</h4>
<p>No. Only the primary channel owner can complete phone, ID, or video verification. Editors and managers inherit the owner's verification tier through <a href="/glossary/channel-permissions/">channel permissions</a> but can't unlock a higher tier themselves.</p>
<h4>How long does it take to unlock advanced features?</h4>
<p>ID and video verification are typically reviewed within 24 hours. Qualifying through channel history alone doesn't have a fixed timeline — most active, policy-compliant channels qualify automatically over time, and YouTube cites roughly 2 months as a typical benchmark for <em>rebuilding</em> access after it's been lost.</p>
<h4>Can YouTube take advanced features away?</h4>
<p>Yes. Community Guidelines strikes, copyright strikes, spam behavior, or other policy violations can restrict advanced feature access. It can be restored by rebuilding channel history or re-verifying with ID or video.</p>
