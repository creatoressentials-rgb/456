<h2>Comment Moderation</h2>
<p><strong>YouTube comment moderation</strong> is the system of settings, automated filters, review queues, moderators, and creator actions used to manage comments on videos, Shorts, posts, and related channel surfaces.</p>
<p>Moderation can control whether comments are allowed, who may comment, which comments are held before publication, and what happens to individual comments or users.</p>

<h3>Main Comment Availability Settings</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Setting</th><th>What Happens</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>On</td><td>New comments are allowed under the selected moderation and participant rules</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Pause</td><td>Existing comments remain visible, but the video temporarily stops accepting new comments</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Off</td><td>Viewers cannot comment and existing comments are hidden until comments are turned on again</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>Comment Moderation Levels</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Level</th><th>Current YouTube Behavior</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>None</td><td>Do not hold comments through the selected moderation level</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Basic</td><td>Hold comments YouTube identifies as potentially inappropriate</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Strict</td><td>Hold a broader range of potentially inappropriate comments</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Hold all</td><td>Hold every new comment until the creator approves it</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>Basic and Strict use automated systems and may make mistakes. The creator remains responsible for reviewing and deciding what should be approved or removed.</p>

<h3>What Can Basic Moderation Hold?</h3>
<p>YouTube says Basic can hold comments that may involve:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Spam</li>
  <li>Self-promotion</li>
  <li>Gibberish</li>
  <li>Potentially inappropriate language or behavior</li>
</ul>
<p>Strict increases the number and range of comments held. YouTube does not publish a complete word list or detection formula.</p>

<h3>How Long Are Held Comments Kept?</h3>
<p>Held comments remain in YouTube Studio for up to 60 days. They are not publicly visible unless approved.</p>
<p>YouTube currently supports held-comment review in more than 100 languages.</p>

<h3>Where to Review Comments</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Community</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose the <strong>Published</strong> or <strong>Held</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Use filters for questions, response status, search, subscribers, and other available criteria.</li>
  <li>Approve, remove, report, hide the user, or take another permitted action.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Actions on Held Comments</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Approve:</strong> Publish the comment.</li>
  <li><strong>Remove:</strong> Delete it from the channel.</li>
  <li><strong>Report:</strong> Send it to YouTube for spam or abuse review.</li>
  <li><strong>Hide user:</strong> Apply a channel-wide user restriction.</li>
</ul>
<p>Ignoring a held comment leaves it unpublished until it expires from the held queue.</p>

<h3>Published Comment Actions</h3>
<p>Depending on the creator's role and interface, published comments can be:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Replied to</li>
  <li>Hearted</li>
  <li>Liked or disliked</li>
  <li>Pinned</li>
  <li>Removed</li>
  <li>Reported</li>
  <li>Used to hide the commenter</li>
  <li>Marked for automatic future approval</li>
</ul>

<h3>Always Approve a User</h3>
<p>The Published tab includes an <strong>Always approve comments from this user</strong> option. Future comments from approved users publish automatically and bypass filters for blocked words, blocked links, and potentially inappropriate content.</p>
<p>Use this status only for trusted people. Approval can allow harmful content through filters that apply to ordinary viewers.</p>

<h3>Subscriber and Member Comment Restrictions</h3>
<p>For an individual video, creators can limit commenting to subscribers and paid members.</p>
<p>Current minimum subscription-time options include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Any duration</li>
  <li>1 hour</li>
  <li>1 day</li>
  <li>1 week</li>
</ul>
<p>YouTube says this participant restriction is not available as a single channel-level default, although creators can use bulk edits for multiple videos.</p>

<h3>Channel Defaults vs. Existing Videos</h3>
<p>Creators can set default comment rules for new uploads. Changing a default does not automatically update existing videos.</p>
<p>After upload, each video can use its own comment setting. Bulk edits can update multiple existing videos when needed.</p>

<h3>Channel-Level Controls</h3>
<p>Channel-wide moderation controls include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Hidden users</li>
  <li>Approved users</li>
  <li>Blocked words and phrases</li>
  <li>Blocked links</li>
  <li>Comment moderators</li>
  <li>Community moderation settings</li>
</ul>
<p>Blocked words, hidden users, and link blocking cannot be limited to one specific video under YouTube's current system.</p>

<h3>Blocked Links</h3>
<p>Creators can hold comments containing URLs for review. Live chat messages containing URLs can also be blocked.</p>
<p>YouTube says link comments are not blocked when posted by:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The channel owner</li>
  <li>Moderators</li>
  <li>Approved users</li>
</ul>
<p>Comments with links or hashtags can remain in Held for up to 60 days.</p>

<h3>Standard vs. Managing Comment Moderators</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Capability</th><th>Managing Moderator</th><th>Standard Moderator</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Hold comments for owner review</td><td>Yes</td><td>Yes</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Report comments</td><td>Yes</td><td>Yes</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Hide or unhide users</td><td>Yes</td><td>Yes</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Manage standard moderators</td><td>Yes</td><td>No</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Manage approved users</td><td>Yes</td><td>No</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Manage blocked words and links</td><td>Yes</td><td>No</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Pin comments</td><td>No</td><td>No</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>Managing moderators have broader authority, but comment moderators do not receive general access to the channel in YouTube Studio.</p>

<h3>Moderator Hold vs. Creator Delete</h3>
<p>YouTube's current mobile permission table distinguishes the actions:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The channel owner can delete a comment.</li>
  <li>Managing and standard comment moderators can hold it for the owner's review.</li>
</ul>
<p>This protects the owner from moderators permanently deleting comments through ordinary comment-moderator access.</p>

<h3>Comment Moderation vs. Live Chat Moderation</h3>
<p>The systems overlap but are not identical. Live chat adds tools such as:</p>
<ul>
  <li>User timeouts</li>
  <li>Message removal during a broadcast</li>
  <li>Slow Mode</li>
  <li>Participant modes</li>
  <li>Live commentary access</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not assume a video-comment permission automatically provides every live-chat action.</p>

<h3>Comment Moderation vs. Channel Guidelines</h3>
<p>Channel guidelines tell viewers what kind of discussion the creator expects. YouTube says the guidelines do not automatically hide or remove comments, posts, or live chat messages.</p>
<p>Guidelines set expectations; moderation settings enforce publication and user controls.</p>

<h3>Comment Search and AI Tools</h3>
<p>YouTube Studio provides tools to find and understand comments, including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Search by topic or meaning</li>
  <li>Suggested comment topics</li>
  <li>Find similar comments</li>
  <li>AI-generated comment summaries</li>
  <li>Suggested replies</li>
  <li>Suggested comments to heart</li>
</ul>
<p>These tools support workflow but do not replace moderation decisions.</p>

<h3>Current Semantic Comment Search Limits</h3>
<p>YouTube's computer Studio search can find comments from the last 90 days by topic or intent. Current limitations include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Queries of up to 100 characters</li>
  <li>No search for comments from one specific user</li>
  <li>The creator's own channel comments are excluded from results</li>
  <li>Existing moderation controls must still be used for action</li>
</ul>

<h3>Pause vs. Hold All</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Setting</th><th>New Viewer Experience</th><th>Creator Workload</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Pause</td><td>Cannot submit new comments temporarily</td><td>No new queue grows</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Hold all</td><td>Can submit, but comments remain invisible until approved</td><td>Every new comment requires review</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>Moderating Criticism</h3>
<p>Negative feedback is not automatically abuse. A useful policy can distinguish among:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Respectful criticism</li>
  <li>Incorrect information</li>
  <li>Personal attacks</li>
  <li>Spam</li>
  <li>Threats</li>
  <li>Harassment</li>
  <li>Off-topic promotion</li>
</ul>
<p>Removing every disagreement can damage trust and deprive the creator of useful feedback.</p>

<h3>Building a Moderation Policy</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Write clear channel guidelines.</li>
  <li>Choose Basic or Strict based on risk and comment volume.</li>
  <li>Add carefully selected blocked words.</li>
  <li>Decide when links should be held.</li>
  <li>Identify trusted approved users.</li>
  <li>Assign moderators with the minimum necessary authority.</li>
  <li>Review Held comments regularly.</li>
  <li>Document when to remove, report, or hide a user.</li>
  <li>Adjust after observing false positives and missed abuse.</li>
</ol>

<h3>High-Risk Situations</h3>
<p>Creators may temporarily increase moderation during:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Viral traffic spikes</li>
  <li>Controversial topics</li>
  <li>Harassment campaigns</li>
  <li>Giveaways</li>
  <li>News events</li>
  <li>Videos involving minors</li>
  <li>Product or company disputes</li>
</ul>
<p>Pause can provide breathing room without permanently deleting existing discussion.</p>

<h3>Common Comment-Moderation Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Assuming Basic catches every harmful comment.</li>
  <li>Using Hold all without enough review capacity.</li>
  <li>Changing defaults and expecting existing videos to update.</li>
  <li>Approving users without understanding filter bypasses.</li>
  <li>Giving managing-moderator access unnecessarily.</li>
  <li>Confusing Pause with Off or Hold all.</li>
  <li>Using hidden-user actions for one isolated comment.</li>
  <li>Treating criticism as a policy violation.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>What is the difference between Basic and Strict?</h4>
<p>Basic holds potentially inappropriate comments; Strict holds a broader range.</p>

<h4>How long do held comments remain?</h4>
<p>YouTube keeps them in Studio for up to 60 days.</p>

<h4>Can comment moderators delete comments?</h4>
<p>Current comment-moderator permissions let them hold comments for owner review; the owner performs deletion through the ordinary role model.</p>

<h4>Do channel guidelines automatically block comments?</h4>
<p>No. They communicate expectations but do not automatically hide or remove content.</p>
