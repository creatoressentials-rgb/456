<h2>YouTube Gifts</h2>
<p><strong>YouTube Gifts</strong> — officially called "Gifts, powered by Jewels" in YouTube's own Help Center — are virtual items that viewers buy and send to creators during eligible live streams. Viewers pay with <strong>Jewels</strong> (an in-app currency), the Gift appears as an animated overlay on top of the stream, and the creator earns <strong>Rubies</strong>, which convert to real money at a fixed rate of 1 Ruby = $0.01.</p>
<p>If you've also seen the term "YouTube gifts" used for letting viewers pay for someone else's <a href="/glossary/channel-memberships/">Channel Memberships</a>, that's a different, older feature — officially called <strong>gift memberships</strong> or <strong>membership gifting</strong>. The two get mixed up constantly across guides and forums, so this entry covers both and is explicit about which is which.</p>

<h3>What "YouTube Gifts" actually refers to today</h3>
<p>When YouTube itself uses the word "Gifts" in Studio, Help Center articles, and live stream UI, it means the Jewels-powered live gifting system — not gift memberships. Key facts:</p>
<ul>
<li>Gifts are purchased with <a href="/glossary/youtube-jewels/">YouTube Jewels</a>, which viewers buy inside the YouTube mobile app (Android, iPhone, iPad).</li>
<li>Sending a Gift shows an animated overlay directly on top of the live stream — not just in the chat panel, the way <a href="/glossary/super-chat/">Super Chat</a> and <a href="/glossary/super-stickers/">Super Stickers</a> messages do.</li>
<li>Creators are paid in Rubies, redeemable at $0.01 each. Earnings show up in YouTube Analytics under Revenue → How you make money → Gifts.</li>
<li>Gifts launched in November 2024 for eligible US creators on vertical live streams, initially replacing Super Stickers there, and has since expanded to more countries and gift types.</li>
</ul>

<h3>YouTube Gifts vs. Gifted Channel Memberships vs. YouTube Jewels</h3>
<p>These three terms are related but not interchangeable. Here's the precise relationship:</p>
<table>
<thead><tr><th></th><th>YouTube Gifts (powered by Jewels)</th><th>Gifted Channel Memberships</th><th>YouTube Jewels</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>What it is</td><td>The live-stream virtual gifting feature itself</td><td>Buying someone else access to your Channel Memberships perks</td><td>The currency used to buy and send Gifts</td></tr>
<tr><td>What's exchanged</td><td>Jewels → Gifts → Rubies</td><td>Cash → 1 month of membership perks</td><td>Cash → Jewels</td></tr>
<tr><td>Where it shows up</td><td>Overlay on top of eligible vertical/horizontal live streams</td><td>Announcement in live chat during a live stream or Premiere</td><td>Purchased in the mobile app; spent on Gifts</td></tr>

</tbody>
</table>
<p>In short: <strong>Jewels are the currency, Gifts are what you buy and send with it, and Rubies are what the creator ends up with.</strong> Gifted Channel Memberships is a completely separate mechanic that extends the Memberships subscription product — it doesn't touch Jewels or Rubies at all.</p>

<h3>How YouTube Gifts work, step by step</h3>
<p><strong>For viewers:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Open a vertical or horizontal live stream in the YouTube mobile app where the creator has Gifts enabled (look for the Gift icon).</li>
<li>Browse the available gifts and pick one.</li>
<li>If you have enough Jewels, the gift sends immediately and appears as an overlay on the stream.</li>
<li>If you don't have enough Jewels, you're prompted to buy a bundle before sending.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>For creators — turning Gifts on:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>In YouTube Studio, go to Earn in the left menu.</li>
<li>Click the Supers &amp; gifts tab.</li>
<li>Scroll to "Turn on gifts in live streams" and select Get Started.</li>
<li>Accept the Virtual Items (VI) Module terms.</li>
<li>Gifts are then automatically enabled on your eligible vertical and horizontal live streams.</li>
</ol>
<p>(On mobile: Studio app → Earn → Gifts → Turn on, then accept the VI Module.)</p>

<h3>Eligibility and where Gifts don't work</h3>
<p>To receive Gifts, your channel needs to meet the Virtual Items program policies and be part of the <a href="/glossary/youtube-partner-program/">YouTube Partner Program</a>. Even if your channel qualifies, Gifts won't appear on:</p>
<ul>
<li>Age-restricted videos</li>
<li>Unlisted or private videos</li>
<li><a href="/glossary/made-for-kids/">Made for Kids</a> videos</li>
<li>Streams with a YouTube Giving fundraiser attached</li>
<li>Any stream with live chat or comments turned off</li>
</ul>
<p>Gifts availability also depends on country: YouTube maintains a specific list of countries where creators can enable Gifts and where viewers can buy Jewels, and it's grown considerably since the November 2024 launch.</p>
<p><strong>A note on horizontal live streams:</strong> YouTube's own Help Center is inconsistent here. Some official pages describe Gifts as available on "eligible vertical and horizontal" live streams, while another official eligibility page lists horizontal live streams under formats where Gifts aren't available. The safest move is to check the Earn hub in your own Studio account — if the Gifts option shows up for a given stream format, it's enabled for you.</p>

<h3>Gift types</h3>
<p>YouTube's Gifts feature currently includes four categories, per YouTube's own "Gift types" documentation:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Standard gifts</strong> — the core gifts viewers send with Jewels; some include a brief interactive animation, marked with a purple sparkle icon.</li>
<li><strong>Gift effects</strong> — effects that blend into the creator's real environment on screen; began rolling out August 28, 2025, and reached all eligible US channels by September 22, 2025.</li>
<li><strong>Limited-time gifts</strong> — seasonal or event-themed gifts with unique designs.</li>
<li><strong>Gift combos</strong> — a pre-packaged sequence viewers can keep tapping to send the same gift repeatedly, with a visible combo counter for the whole chat.</li>
</ul>

<h3>What Gifts mean for growing your channel</h3>
<p>Gifts are best thought of as another live-stream monetization layer alongside <a href="/glossary/super-chat/">Super Chat</a>, <a href="/glossary/super-stickers/">Super Stickers</a>, <a href="/glossary/super-thanks/">Super Thanks</a>, and <a href="/glossary/ad-revenue/">ad revenue</a> — not a replacement for any of them. A few practical points for creators:</p>
<ul>
<li>Because Gifts appear as on-screen overlays rather than chat-only messages, they tend to reward creators who stream in a visually engaging, reactive format — viewers are literally sending something that shows up on your video, not just in a side panel.</li>
<li>Gifts has no published fixed revenue-share percentage, unlike Super Chat, which makes it harder to forecast earnings precisely — budget for it as a bonus stream, not a predictable line item alongside your <a href="/glossary/youtube-rpm/">YouTube RPM</a>.</li>
<li>Since Gifts is only one input to your live-stream income, pair it with a broader <a href="/glossary/fan-funding/">fan funding</a> strategy rather than relying on it alone.</li>
<li>Distribution of gifted Channel Memberships specifically favors your most loyal, <a href="/glossary/returning-viewers/">returning viewers</a> — creators who consistently convert casual watchers into <a href="/glossary/regular-viewers/">regular viewers</a> through community-focused live streams tend to see more of both gifting activity and membership growth.</li>
</ul>

<h3>FAQ</h3>
<h4>Is "YouTube Gifts" the same as "gifted memberships"?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube Gifts (powered by Jewels) is a live-stream tipping system where viewers buy Jewels and send virtual items that pay creators in Rubies. Gifted memberships is a separate feature where someone pays for another viewer to get one month of your <a href="/glossary/channel-memberships/">Channel Memberships</a> perks. They use different currencies, different payout mechanics, and appear differently on screen.</p>
<h4>Do I need to be in the YouTube Partner Program to receive Gifts?</h4>
<p>Yes. Your channel needs to meet YouTube's Virtual Items program policies, which sit under the broader <a href="/glossary/youtube-partner-program/">YouTube Partner Program</a> monetization requirements, and you must accept the Virtual Items (VI) Module in Studio before Gifts can be turned on.</p>
<h4>Why don't I see the Gifts option in YouTube Studio?</h4>
<p>The most common reasons are: your channel or country isn't yet eligible for the feature, you haven't accepted the Virtual Items Module, or the specific video is ineligible (age-restricted, unlisted, private, Made for Kids, has a YouTube Giving fundraiser attached, or has live chat/comments turned off). Availability is also still expanding by country, so a channel that qualifies for the Partner Program overall may not yet have Gifts access.</p>
