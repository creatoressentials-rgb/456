<h2>Gift Membership</h2>
<p>A <strong>YouTube gift membership</strong> lets a creator or viewer purchase the opportunity for other eligible viewers to receive one month of a channel's membership perks.</p>
<p>Gift memberships are commonly purchased during an eligible live stream or Premiere. YouTube distributes them to viewers who have opted in and meet the platform's eligibility rules.</p>

<h3>What Does a Gift Membership Include?</h3>
<p>A recipient can receive up to one month of access to the applicable channel membership perks, which may include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Loyalty badges</li>
  <li>Custom emoji</li>
  <li>Members-only posts</li>
  <li>Members-only videos or live streams</li>
  <li>Member chat privileges</li>
  <li>Other creator-provided perks included at the gifted level</li>
</ul>
<p>The exact perks depend on the channel's membership offer. The recipient does not choose a custom perk package.</p>

<h3>Who Can Buy Gift Memberships?</h3>
<p>When gifting is enabled, the channel creator or eligible viewers can buy memberships during a live stream or Premiere.</p>
<p>The purchase flow generally involves:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Opening an eligible live stream or Premiere.</li>
  <li>Opening live chat.</li>
  <li>Selecting Membership gifting.</li>
  <li>Choosing the number of memberships to gift.</li>
  <li>Completing the transaction.</li>
</ol>
<p>Feature availability can depend on country, platform, account, channel eligibility, and membership status.</p>

<h3>What Membership Level Is Gifted?</h3>
<p>YouTube says gift memberships are offered at the closest highest membership price level available up to and including $9.99.</p>
<p>This means the gift does not automatically provide the channel's most expensive tier. The platform selects the applicable supported gifting level under its current pricing rules.</p>

<h3>Who Can Receive a Gift Membership?</h3>
<p>Under YouTube's current rules, a viewer must:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Opt in to allow gift memberships</li>
  <li>Subscribe to the channel</li>
  <li>Have interacted with the channel recently</li>
  <li>Use a YouTube channel that is not a Brand Account</li>
  <li>Not already be an active channel member</li>
  <li>Be in a country, account, and platform context where gifting is available</li>
</ul>
<p>Opting in globally makes a viewer eligible to receive gifts from channels with gifting enabled that the viewer subscribes to and has interacted with recently.</p>

<h3>Can the Purchaser Choose the Recipient?</h3>
<p>No. The purchaser buys a quantity of gift memberships, and YouTube distributes them among eligible opted-in viewers. The purchaser cannot normally select a specific friend, chat participant, or username.</p>
<p>YouTube's distribution system can consider factors such as viewer interaction and channel activity, but the platform does not provide the purchaser with a recipient list to control.</p>

<h3>How Do Viewers Opt In?</h3>
<p>Eligible viewers can opt in through:</p>
<ul>
  <li>An Allow Gifts prompt in a live chat</li>
  <li>A pinned Membership gifting message</li>
  <li>Gift Settings from an eligible channel or video watch page</li>
  <li>A creator's dedicated allow-gifts URL</li>
</ul>
<p>Viewers can later turn Allow gifts off to stop being eligible for gifts from any channel.</p>

<h3>What Happens When Gifts Are Purchased?</h3>
<ol>
  <li>A countdown ticker highlights the purchase in live chat.</li>
  <li>YouTube distributes the memberships to eligible viewers.</li>
  <li>Selected recipients see a live-chat notification and receive an email.</li>
  <li>The recipients receive access to perks for up to one month.</li>
  <li>The memberships expire without an automatic recurring charge.</li>
</ol>
<p>If the stream or chat ends before the gift announcement finishes, YouTube says the memberships will still be distributed.</p>

<h3>Are Gift Memberships Recurring?</h3>
<p>No. Gift memberships expire after up to one month and do not automatically convert into paid recurring memberships.</p>
<p>The recipient can choose to purchase a normal channel membership separately, but YouTube does not automatically bill the recipient when the gift ends.</p>

<h3>Are Gift Memberships Refundable?</h3>
<p>YouTube says gift memberships are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash value. The purchase is considered fulfilled once YouTube distributes the first membership from the purchase to a viewer.</p>
<p>The purchaser should understand that:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Recipients are selected by YouTube.</li>
  <li>Not every gift may be announced before the live event ends.</li>
  <li>The gift is not a promise that a specific person will receive access.</li>
  <li>Platform purchase and refund rules can vary by device and country.</li>
</ul>

<h3>What Information Is Public?</h3>
<p>YouTube says the number of gifted memberships, the purchaser's channel name, and profile picture are visible publicly. A selected recipient's channel name can also become public.</p>
<p>This information may be available to the creator through the YouTube Data API, and the channel can share it with third-party services. Gift purchasers and recipients should not assume the transaction is anonymous.</p>

<h3>How Do Creators Earn From Paid Gift Memberships?</h3>
<p>Paid gift memberships are part of the channel memberships system. YouTube's current membership revenue guidance says creators receive 70% of membership revenue recognized by Google after local sales tax and other applicable fees are deducted. YouTube currently covers payment-processing fees such as credit-card fees.</p>
<p>The exact amount received from a gift purchase can vary because:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Membership pricing varies by country and platform.</li>
  <li>Local sales taxes can be deducted.</li>
  <li>App Store or platform fees can apply.</li>
  <li>Currency conversion and recognized revenue can differ.</li>
  <li>Refunds and adjustments can affect finalized earnings.</li>
</ul>

<h3>What Are Promotional Gift Memberships?</h3>
<p>YouTube may provide eligible creators with promotional gift memberships to distribute. Current guidance says a channel may receive them when it has a membership tier below $5.</p>
<p>Promotional gifts differ from purchased gifts:</p>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Paid Gift Membership</th><th>Promotional Gift Membership</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Who funds it?</td><td>Creator or viewer purchase</td><td>YouTube promotional allocation</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Creator revenue</td><td>Normal applicable membership revenue share</td><td>No creator revenue</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Viewer charge</td><td>Purchaser pays</td><td>No charge to creator or recipient</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Carryover</td><td>Purchased during the event</td><td>Monthly promotional gifts do not carry over</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Current limitation</td><td>Subject to gifting availability</td><td>Not currently available to Brand Accounts</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>YouTube says creators must distribute available promotional gifts before buying additional memberships to gift to viewers.</p>

<h3>Can Creators Gift Their Own Memberships?</h3>
<p>Yes, when the channel and account are eligible. Creators can purchase gifts during their own live streams or Premieres. Eligible creators may also receive YouTube-funded promotional gifts.</p>
<p>A creator buying memberships is making a real purchase unless the interface specifically identifies the gifts as promotional and no-cost.</p>

<h3>Gift Memberships and the Commerce Product Module</h3>
<p>Channel memberships and related gifting are fan-funding features. Creators must accept the applicable Commerce Product Module or legacy Commerce Product Addendum and meet the individual channel-membership requirements.</p>
<p>The module does not guarantee gifting availability on every device, country, video, or account.</p>

<h3>Where Can Creators See Gift Analytics?</h3>
<p>YouTube's membership analytics can show gift-membership activity. Creators can select Memberships Type to review how many memberships were gifted and redeemed during the selected period.</p>
<p>Creators should distinguish:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Memberships purchased as gifts</li>
  <li>Memberships successfully distributed or redeemed</li>
  <li>Normal recurring paid memberships</li>
  <li>Promotional gifts that generate no revenue</li>
</ul>

<h3>Gift Memberships vs. Recurring Memberships</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Gift Membership</th><th>Recurring Channel Membership</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Payment</td><td>Paid by another viewer, the creator, or a promotion</td><td>Paid by the member</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Duration</td><td>Up to one month</td><td>Renews monthly until canceled or terminated</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Recipient choice</td><td>Selected by YouTube from eligible opted-in viewers</td><td>The viewer joins directly</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Automatic renewal</td><td>No</td><td>Yes</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>Common Gift-Membership Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Assuming the purchaser can choose a specific recipient.</li>
  <li>Believing all subscribers are automatically eligible.</li>
  <li>Forgetting viewers must opt in.</li>
  <li>Assuming current members can receive another gift.</li>
  <li>Treating gifts as recurring subscriptions.</li>
  <li>Assuming promotional gifts generate creator revenue.</li>
  <li>Expecting the gift to provide the highest channel tier.</li>
  <li>Assuming the purchaser and recipient remain anonymous.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>How long does a YouTube gift membership last?</h4>
<p>It provides up to one month of access and then expires without automatically charging the recipient.</p>

<h4>Can I gift a membership to one specific person?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube distributes purchased gifts among eligible viewers who have opted in.</p>

<h4>Do recipients have to subscribe?</h4>
<p>Yes. YouTube's current eligibility rules require recipients to subscribe and have interacted with the channel recently.</p>

<h4>Do creators earn from promotional gift memberships?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says promotional gifts are free to distribute and do not generate creator revenue.</p>
