<h2>YouTube Poll</h2>
<p>A <strong>YouTube poll</strong> is an interactive question that lets viewers choose from multiple response options. Creators can use polls in YouTube posts, live chat, Shorts interactive stickers, and members-only posts where those features are available.</p>
<p>The term does not refer to one single poll format. A post poll, live poll, and Shorts poll sticker appear in different places and follow different creation and voting rules.</p>

<h3>Main Types of YouTube Polls</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Poll Type</th><th>Where It Appears</th><th>Best Use</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Text post poll</td><td>Posts tab, channel Home shelf, Home feed, and Subscriptions feed</td><td>Quick audience choices and topic research</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Image post poll</td><td>The same post-distribution surfaces with an image for each choice</td><td>Visual comparisons, designs, thumbnails, and products</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Live poll</td><td>Live chat during a live stream or Premiere</td><td>Real-time audience participation</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Shorts poll sticker</td><td>Inside an eligible Short</td><td>Interactive questions connected to the video itself</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Members-only poll</td><td>A members-only post</td><td>Decisions and feedback from paying channel members</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>How Post Polls Work</h3>
<p>Post polls are created through YouTube's post composer. YouTube currently supports:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Text polls:</strong> A question with written response options</li>
  <li><strong>Image polls:</strong> A question with images and short captions as the options</li>
</ul>
<p>A post poll can be published immediately or scheduled for a future date and time.</p>

<h3>Current Post Poll Limits</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Element</th><th>Current Limit</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Answer options</td><td>Up to four</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Text-poll answer length</td><td>Up to 65 characters per option</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Image-poll caption length</td><td>Up to 36 characters per option</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>The question should be understandable without requiring viewers to open another video or page.</p>

<h3>How to Create a Text or Image Poll</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube or YouTube Studio where post creation is available.</li>
  <li>Start a new post.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Poll</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose a text poll or image poll.</li>
  <li>Enter the question.</li>
  <li>Add two to four clear answer options.</li>
  <li>For an image poll, upload an image and add a caption for each option.</li>
  <li>Publish immediately or schedule the post.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Where Can Viewers Find Post Polls?</h3>
<p>YouTube says posts may appear:</p>
<ul>
  <li>On the channel's Posts tab</li>
  <li>On the Posts shelf of the channel's Home tab</li>
  <li>On viewers' Home pages</li>
  <li>In viewers' Subscriptions feeds</li>
  <li>Through subscriber notifications in some cases</li>
</ul>
<p>Publishing a poll does not guarantee that every subscriber will see it.</p>

<h3>YouTube Poll vs. YouTube Quiz</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Poll</th><th>Quiz</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Purpose</td><td>Collect opinions or preferences</td><td>Test knowledge</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Correct answer</td><td>No required correct choice</td><td>One correct answer must be selected</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Explanation</td><td>No quiz-style answer explanation</td><td>Optional explanation of up to 500 characters</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Typical use</td><td>Vote on topics, designs, or preferences</td><td>Education, trivia, and knowledge checks</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>Post Poll vs. Live Poll</h3>
<p>A live poll is created inside live chat and is designed for immediate interaction during a broadcast. It is separate from a post poll.</p>
<p>Live polls can be used to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Choose the next discussion topic</li>
  <li>Collect predictions</li>
  <li>Check whether viewers understand an explanation</li>
  <li>Select a challenge or activity</li>
  <li>Gather feedback before the stream ends</li>
</ul>
<p>The creator can end a live poll and display the result in chat. Live-poll voting rules should not automatically be applied to post polls or Shorts poll stickers.</p>

<h3>Post Poll vs. Shorts Poll Sticker</h3>
<p>A Shorts poll sticker is placed inside the Short and remains connected to that video. It is designed for viewers interacting while watching the Short.</p>
<p>Current YouTube guidance for Shorts poll stickers says the poll remains open while the Short is published and viewers can change their vote. This differs from YouTube's current live-poll guidance, which says a viewer cannot change a submitted live-poll vote.</p>

<h3>Public Polls vs. Members-Only Polls</h3>
<p>Channels with memberships can publish members-only posts that include polls. This allows the creator to ask paying members about:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Upcoming exclusive content</li>
  <li>Live-stream times</li>
  <li>Membership perk preferences</li>
  <li>Behind-the-scenes choices</li>
  <li>Which topic should receive early access</li>
</ul>
<p>A members-only poll is a post-access setting, not a different poll engine.</p>

<h3>Can Viewers Create Polls?</h3>
<p>Channels using YouTube Communities can allow eligible subscribed viewers to create Community posts, including polls. The creator can control eligibility and moderation for the Community.</p>
<p>Viewer Community polls are separate from the creator's standard Posts tab and may be restricted to the Community context under the current YouTube Communities design.</p>

<h3>Who Can Create Post Polls?</h3>
<p>Post access depends on channel and account eligibility. YouTube currently says posts are unavailable when:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The creator uses a supervised account</li>
  <li>The channel is set as <a href="/glossary/made-for-kids/">Made for Kids</a></li>
  <li>The feature is unavailable in the creator's location or account context</li>
  <li>YouTube has restricted the channel's feature access</li>
</ul>
<p>YouTube's current Help page also notes that posts are not presently available in Germany.</p>

<h3>Can a Poll Be Scheduled?</h3>
<p>Yes. YouTube supports scheduled posts. The creator can choose Schedule Post and select a future date and time.</p>
<p>Scheduling can help coordinate a poll with:</p>
<ul>
  <li>An upcoming upload</li>
  <li>A live stream</li>
  <li>A product launch</li>
  <li>A weekly audience routine</li>
  <li>A membership content calendar</li>
</ul>

<h3>Are YouTube Polls Scientific Surveys?</h3>
<p>No. Poll respondents are a self-selected group of viewers who saw the post and chose to vote.</p>
<p>Results can be influenced by:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Which viewers YouTube showed the poll to</li>
  <li>Subscriber and non-subscriber reach</li>
  <li>Publication time</li>
  <li>Question wording</li>
  <li>Option order</li>
  <li>Existing audience loyalty</li>
  <li>Whether the poll was public or members-only</li>
</ul>
<p>Use poll results as audience feedback rather than a statistically representative market study.</p>

<h3>How to Write a Good Poll Question</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Ask one clear question.</li>
  <li>Make the options mutually understandable.</li>
  <li>Avoid combining two questions in one option.</li>
  <li>Use neutral wording when genuine research matters.</li>
  <li>Include an Other option when important choices may be missing.</li>
  <li>Keep the decision connected to the channel.</li>
  <li>Do not promise that the winning option will definitely happen unless you intend to follow through.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Polls and Content Strategy</h3>
<p>A poll can help identify audience language and preferences, but it should be combined with:</p>
<ul>
  <li><a href="/glossary/youtube-search-terms/">YouTube Search Terms</a></li>
  <li><a href="/glossary/youtube-trends-tab/">YouTube Trends Tab</a></li>
  <li>Video comments</li>
  <li>Audience retention</li>
  <li>Actual viewing behavior</li>
  <li>Business and production constraints</li>
</ul>
<p>What viewers say they want and what they later watch can differ.</p>

<h3>Polls and Thumbnail Research</h3>
<p>Image polls can gather preferences between visual concepts, but they are not a controlled substitute for native <a href="/glossary/youtube-a-b-testing/">YouTube A/B Testing</a>.</p>
<p>Poll voters know they are comparing images, and the post audience may differ from the viewers who encounter the actual video. Use poll feedback for qualitative direction rather than claiming one image has proven a higher click-through rate.</p>

<h3>Poll Engagement Does Not Equal Video Demand</h3>
<p>A playful poll can receive many votes because it is easy to answer. A complex video topic may receive fewer poll votes but generate more valuable watch time, search demand, or customer interest.</p>
<p>Compare poll participation with actual content performance before changing the channel strategy.</p>

<h3>Poll Policies</h3>
<p>Poll questions, options, and images must follow YouTube's Community Guidelines. Polls cannot be used to distribute prohibited content, harass people, manipulate engagement, or mislead viewers.</p>
<p>Images should be owned, licensed, or otherwise used with permission.</p>

<h3>Common YouTube-Poll Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Confusing polls with quizzes.</li>
  <li>Using more than four answer choices.</li>
  <li>Treating results as scientific market research.</li>
  <li>Writing biased or overlapping options.</li>
  <li>Assuming every subscriber saw the poll.</li>
  <li>Calling an image poll a controlled thumbnail test.</li>
  <li>Applying live-poll voting rules to Shorts poll stickers.</li>
  <li>Ignoring the difference between public, Community-only, and members-only posts.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>How many options can a YouTube post poll have?</h4>
<p>YouTube currently allows up to four options.</p>

<h4>Can YouTube polls use images?</h4>
<p>Yes. Creators can make an image poll with up to four image choices and short captions.</p>

<h4>Can a poll be members-only?</h4>
<p>Yes. Eligible channels can include polls in members-only posts.</p>

<h4>Can viewers change their vote?</h4>
<p>The answer depends on the poll type. Current Shorts poll-sticker guidance allows vote changes, while current live-poll guidance says submitted votes cannot be changed.</p>
