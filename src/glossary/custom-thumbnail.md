<h2>Custom Thumbnail</h2>
<p>A <strong>custom thumbnail</strong> is an image a creator uploads to represent a YouTube video instead of selecting one of YouTube's automatically generated frames.</p>
<p>The thumbnail appears in many places where viewers decide whether to watch, including Home, Search, Suggested videos, channel pages, watch history, subscriptions, and embedded players. Exact display shape and cropping can vary by surface and device.</p>

<h3>Custom Thumbnail vs. Auto-Generated Thumbnail</h3>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Thumbnail Type</th><th>Source</th><th>Creator Control</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Custom thumbnail</td><td>An uploaded image designed or selected by the creator</td><td>High control over subject, composition, text, and visual promise</td></tr>
<tr><td>Auto-generated thumbnail</td><td>A frame YouTube selects from the uploaded video</td><td>Creator chooses among the suggested frames</td></tr>
<tr><td>Shorts selected frame</td><td>A frame selected from the Short</td><td>Different workflow and placement rules from long-form custom thumbnails</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h3>Who Can Upload a Custom Thumbnail?</h3>
<p>YouTube says creators can upload their own custom thumbnail when the account is verified and the channel has the required feature access.</p>
<p>This type of account verification is not the same as the public verification badge shown next to some large or notable channel names.</p>

<h3>Current Thumbnail Recommendations</h3>
<p>YouTube's current desktop Help guidance recommends that custom thumbnails:</p>
<ul>
<li>Use a resolution of 3840 by 2160 pixels</li>
<li>Have a minimum width of 640 pixels</li>
<li>Use an image format such as JPG, GIF, or PNG</li>
<li>Use a 16:9 aspect ratio for most video thumbnails</li>
<li>Remain within the upload device's file-size limit</li>
</ul>
<p>Current file-size guidance differs by upload device:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mobile:</strong> 2 MB for video thumbnails and 10 MB for podcast thumbnails</li>
<li><strong>Desktop:</strong> 50 MB for video and podcast thumbnails</li>
</ul>
<p>YouTube documentation has recommended different ideal resolutions in the past, which is why older guides often still list 1280 by 720. Always verify the current Help page before building a permanent export preset.</p>

<h3>Why Use a Custom Thumbnail?</h3>
<ul>
<li>Show the video's primary subject clearly.</li>
<li>Communicate an outcome that is difficult to capture in one random frame.</li>
<li>Create readable composition at small sizes.</li>
<li>Coordinate the image with the title.</li>
<li>Build recognizable series or channel branding.</li>
<li>Select a better expression, product angle, or before-and-after view.</li>
<li>Remove distracting motion blur or interface overlays.</li>
</ul>
<p>YouTube says 90% of its best-performing videos have custom thumbnails. That statistic describes an association among high-performing videos; it does not mean uploading any custom image automatically improves performance.</p>

<h3>How to Design a Readable Thumbnail</h3>
<ol>
<li>Choose one primary subject or visual idea.</li>
<li>Make the image understandable at phone size.</li>
<li>Use clear separation between the subject and background.</li>
<li>Keep text short and large enough to read.</li>
<li>Avoid repeating the complete title inside the image.</li>
<li>Use expressions, arrows, circles, and effects only when they add accurate information.</li>
<li>Check how the image works with the actual title.</li>
<li>Preview it on mobile, computer, and television-sized layouts.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Title and Thumbnail Relationship</h3>
<p>The title and thumbnail should work together to create one accurate expectation. They do not need to repeat the same words.</p>
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Thumbnail Role</th><th>Title Role</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Show the product, person, result, or visual problem</td><td>Explain the topic, outcome, or reason to care</td></tr>
<tr><td>Create a clear visual question</td><td>Provide enough context for the question to make sense</td></tr>
<tr><td>Show before-and-after evidence</td><td>Describe what changed or how it happened</td></tr>
<tr><td>Display a recognizable series style</td><td>Identify the specific episode or subject</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h3>Custom Thumbnails for Shorts</h3>
<p>YouTube says creators cannot upload a custom thumbnail for Shorts in the same way they can for long-form videos. Creators can select a frame from the Short for certain surfaces.</p>
<p>Current YouTube Help also notes that vertical videos with a 16:9 custom thumbnail may display an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on mobile Home, Explore, and Subscriptions surfaces, while the custom thumbnail can still appear in other locations.</p>
<p>Because Shorts behavior and surfaces change over time, verify the current upload workflow before relying on a cover image.</p>

<h3>Custom Thumbnail Policies</h3>
<p>Custom thumbnails must follow YouTube's Community Guidelines and thumbnail policies. YouTube prohibits imagery and text involving categories such as pornography, certain nudity and sexual content, shocking violence or gore, vulgar imagery, and thumbnails that mislead viewers about what appears in the video.</p>
<p>Consequences can include thumbnail removal, age restriction, warnings, strikes, temporary loss of thumbnail privileges, or channel termination in severe or repeated cases.</p>

<h3>Can You Use Copyrighted Images?</h3>
<p>Uploading an image as a thumbnail does not remove copyright or trademark obligations. Use images you created, licensed assets, permitted press materials, public-domain works, or material covered by a valid legal exception.</p>
<p>Also avoid impersonating another person, company, or channel or creating false endorsement.</p>

<h3>Thumbnail File and Color Problems</h3>
<ul>
<li>Export in a supported image format.</li>
<li>Use a standard color space such as sRGB for predictable web display.</li>
<li>Avoid fine details that disappear after compression.</li>
<li>Check text and faces after YouTube processes the image.</li>
<li>Allow time for a changed thumbnail to propagate across YouTube.</li>
<li>Keep the project file so the image can be re-exported at a different size.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Daily Custom Thumbnail Limits</h3>
<p>YouTube limits how many custom thumbnails a channel can upload each day. The limit can vary by country, region, and channel history. If the daily limit is reached, YouTube advises waiting 24 hours before trying again.</p>
<p>Copyright and Community Guidelines strikes can affect feature access and limits.</p>

<h3>How to Measure Thumbnail Performance</h3>
<ul>
<li>Review impressions and click-through rate by traffic source.</li>
<li>Check whether the video retains the viewers attracted by the packaging.</li>
<li>Compare Home and Suggested performance for broader audiences.</li>
<li>Review Subscription feed CTR for engaged subscribers.</li>
<li>Use <a href="/glossary/youtube-a-b-testing/">YouTube A/B Testing</a> for a concurrent thumbnail test when eligible.</li>
<li>Test older videos when a new packaging concept may revive discovery.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Common Custom-Thumbnail Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
<li>Using an outdated universal file-size or resolution rule.</li>
<li>Assuming custom thumbnails can be uploaded to Shorts exactly like long-form videos.</li>
<li>Designing only at full monitor size rather than previewing it small.</li>
<li>Repeating the full title as tiny thumbnail text.</li>
<li>Using misleading images that do not appear in or accurately represent the video.</li>
<li>Including copyrighted or trademarked material without permission.</li>
<li>Assuming a custom thumbnail guarantees higher CTR.</li>
<li>Ignoring how different devices and YouTube surfaces crop the image.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>What size should a YouTube custom thumbnail be?</h4>
<p>YouTube's current desktop Help guidance recommends 3840 by 2160 pixels with a minimum width of 640 pixels and a 16:9 aspect ratio for most videos.</p>
<h4>Can any channel upload custom thumbnails?</h4>
<p>The account must be verified and have the required feature access.</p>
<h4>Can I upload a custom thumbnail for a Short?</h4>
<p>Not through the same long-form custom-thumbnail upload process. Shorts use a selected-frame workflow with surface-specific behavior.</p>
<h4>Does a custom thumbnail improve views?</h4>
<p>It can improve communication and packaging, but performance depends on the image, title, topic, audience, competition, and viewer satisfaction.</p>
