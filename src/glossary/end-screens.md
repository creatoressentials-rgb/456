<h2>End Screens</h2>

<h2>What Is a YouTube End Screen?</h2>

<p>A YouTube End Screen is a feature that overlays the last 5 to 20 seconds of a video.</p>

<p>It can contain various elements such as links to other videos or playlists, a 'Subscribe' button, promotions for merchandise, and external website links. The end screen's purpose is to direct viewers to take specific actions after watching a video.</p>

<h2>Are YouTube End Screens Important?</h2>

<p>Absolutely. While they are only one of many factors that contribute to the overall health of a channel, YouTube End Screens are important for several reasons.</p>

<ol>
<li>
<p>User <a href="/glossary/retention/">Retention</a>: They help <a>retain viewers</a> by guiding them to more of your content, preventing them from drifting to other videos or channels.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enhanced Engagement: By presenting options directly related to the content they've just viewed, it enhances user engagement and can lead to increased watch time.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Mobile-Friendly: Unlike the older 'annotations' feature, end screens are mobile-friendly, catering to a significant portion of the YouTube audience.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Strategic Marketing: They act as an effective marketing tool by directing viewers to take specific actions, be it subscribing, visiting an external site, or purchasing merchandise.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>How to Make a YouTube End Screen</h2>

<p><a href="https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en#:~:text=End%20screens%20can%20be%20added,standard%2016%3A9%20aspect%20ratio.">Adding End Screens</a> to your video is a fairly straightforward process.</p>

<ol>
<li>
<p>Sign into your YouTube account.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click on your account icon in the top right corner.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select 'Creator Studio'.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>From the left menu, select 'Video manager' then 'Videos'.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose the video you wish to add an end screen to and click 'Edit'.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Select the 'End screen &amp; Annotations' tab.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Unpublish any annotations (if they exist) on the selected video.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Click on 'Add Element' on the right-hand side.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Choose from the elements: Video/Playlist, Subscribe, Channel, and Link.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>After selecting each element, fill in the required information, and click 'Create Element'.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Adjust the display time of each element using the <a href="/glossary/timeline/">timeline</a> below.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Save changes and optionally preview the video to check the end screen.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>Benefits of YouTube End Screens</h2>

<p>There are several key advantages to adding end screens to each of your videos:</p>

<ol>
<li>
<p>Increased Traffic and Views: End screens can direct viewers to other content on your channel, thus increasing overall views.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Audience Retention: By suggesting more of your content, viewers are likely to stay on your channel longer.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Enhanced Subscriber Growth: By providing a clear 'Subscribe' option, end screens can help increase your subscriber count.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Promotions and Sales: For those selling merchandise or services, end screens can direct viewers to purchase or learn more.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Holistic Content Approach: End screens can be used to interlink videos, creating a comprehensive viewing experience for the audience.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>Tips For End Screens</h2>

<p>Here are helpful tips for making the most out of your End Screens on YouTube:</p>

<ol>
<li>
<p>Clear Labels: Use terms like 'Watch Next' to guide viewers on what action to take.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Add Audio: Reinforce your call-to-action with an audio message during the end screen.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Simplicity is Key: An uncluttered end screen helps viewers focus on the most important elements.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Promote Playlists: Consider featuring a playlist instead of a single video to ensure continuous viewing and more engagement.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p>Remember, the end goal of the end screen is to re-engage your audience, guiding them to take further action, whether that's watching more content, subscribing, or visiting an external link.</p>
