<h2>Loyalty Badge</h2>
<p>A <strong>YouTube loyalty badge</strong> is a small channel-membership icon displayed beside an active member's name in comments, live chat, and channel posts. The badge represents how long the viewer has been an active paid member of that channel.</p>
<p>Creators can use YouTube's default badges, upload custom badges, or combine custom and default designs across the available membership-duration slots.</p>

<h3>Where Do Loyalty Badges Appear?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Comments on the creator's videos and Shorts</li>
  <li>Live chat during live streams and Premieres</li>
  <li>The channel's Posts tab</li>
  <li>Other eligible channel-membership interactions</li>
</ul>
<p>The badge is public-facing. Viewers who see the member's comment or chat message can recognize that the person has an active membership and see the icon associated with the member's current loyalty duration.</p>

<h3>What Does the Badge Measure?</h3>
<p>The badge reflects accumulated active paid membership time—not the member's current price level.</p>
<p>YouTube's current documentation lists duration milestones including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>New member</li>
  <li>1 month</li>
  <li>2 months</li>
  <li>6 months</li>
  <li>1 year</li>
  <li>2 years</li>
  <li>3 years</li>
  <li>4 years</li>
  <li>5 years</li>
</ul>
<p>YouTube's Help text currently describes "eight different badges" while listing nine duration labels. Creators should rely on the badge slots actually displayed in YouTube Studio rather than attempting to reconcile that wording themselves.</p>

<h3>Active Paid Time vs. Calendar Time</h3>
<p>Loyalty is based on the months the viewer actively paid for membership. It is not simply the time since the original Join date.</p>
<p>For example, a viewer who first joined 12 months ago but paid for only nine active months may display the badge associated with nine months of loyalty rather than a one-year badge.</p>

<h3>Does Membership Level Change the Badge?</h3>
<p>No. YouTube says all members receive the same badge based on loyalty. A higher-priced membership level does not receive a different badge solely because the viewer pays more.</p>
<p>A channel can create level-specific perks, but the standard loyalty badge system tracks membership duration across the program.</p>

<h3>What Happens When a Member Changes Levels?</h3>
<p>Changing to a higher or lower membership level does not normally reset accumulated badge loyalty. YouTube's billing guidance says a member who downgrades retains accumulated loyalty.</p>
<p>The viewer's perk access can change at the next billing date, but the badge continues to reflect qualifying membership history.</p>

<h3>What Happens After Cancellation?</h3>
<p>A member who cancels generally keeps the badge and other active perks until the end of the paid billing cycle. After the membership expires, the loyalty badge no longer appears as an active channel-membership benefit.</p>
<p>If the viewer rejoins later, YouTube may restore eligible accumulated loyalty according to its current membership records and billing rules.</p>

<h3>Gift Memberships and Badges</h3>
<p>A viewer who receives a <a href="/glossary/gift-membership/">Gift Membership</a> can use membership perks, including the applicable loyalty badge, during the gift period.</p>
<p>Gift memberships do not automatically renew. When the gifted month ends, badge access ends unless the viewer begins a paid membership or receives another eligible gift.</p>

<h3>How to Upload Custom Loyalty Badges</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio on a computer.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Earn</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open the <strong>Memberships</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Loyalty Badges</strong>.</li>
  <li>Upload an image for each desired duration slot.</li>
  <li>Review the badges at their small preview sizes.</li>
  <li>Save or publish the membership changes.</li>
</ol>

<h3>What Happens to Empty Badge Slots?</h3>
<p>YouTube says an empty duration slot can be filled by:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The highest custom badge the creator uploaded, or</li>
  <li>The default YouTube badge for that duration</li>
</ul>
<p>This allows a creator to launch with only a few custom stages, although a complete progression is usually clearer.</p>

<h3>Current File Specifications</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Requirement</th><th>Current YouTube Specification</th></tr></thead>

<p>The uploaded source can be larger than the rendered display, but detail that looks good at full size may disappear when reduced to 14 or 16 pixels.</p>

<h3>How to Design a Readable Badge</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Use one simple symbol.</li>
  <li>Remove tiny text.</li>
  <li>Use bold shapes and strong contrast.</li>
  <li>Avoid thin outlines.</li>
  <li>Keep important artwork close to the center.</li>
  <li>Limit the number of colors.</li>
  <li>Preview the design at 14 × 14 and 16 × 16 pixels.</li>
  <li>Test it against light and dark backgrounds.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Creating a Loyalty Progression</h3>
<p>A strong badge sequence should become visibly more valuable without changing so much that viewers cannot recognize the underlying channel symbol.</p>
<p>Progression ideas include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Changing material from bronze to silver to gold</li>
  <li>Adding stars or decorative elements</li>
  <li>Growing a plant or character</li>
  <li>Upgrading equipment or armor</li>
  <li>Changing color intensity</li>
  <li>Adding a crown or final-stage accent</li>
</ul>

<h3>Badge Progression vs. Membership Levels</h3>
<p>Badge progression should represent time, while membership levels represent price and perk access.</p>
<p>A member at the least expensive level can earn the five-year badge through long-term support. A brand-new member at the highest level receives the new-member badge.</p>

<h3>Loyalty Badge vs. Verification Badge</h3>
<p>A loyalty badge is a channel-specific membership perk shown beside a member's name on that channel. A verification badge identifies an official or notable channel across YouTube.</p>
<p>Paying for a channel membership does not verify the member's own channel.</p>

<h3>Loyalty Badge vs. Moderator Badge</h3>
<p>Moderator and creator badges identify chat roles. Loyalty badges identify active paid membership duration.</p>
<p>A person can be both a member and a moderator, but YouTube controls how role indicators appear in each interface.</p>

<h3>Can Different Levels Have Different Badges?</h3>
<p>YouTube's standard loyalty system does not assign separate badge sets by membership level. All active members use the same time-based progression.</p>
<p>Creators can differentiate levels through named perks, exclusive content, posts, and live access rather than badge artwork.</p>

<h3>Can a Creator Change the Badge Designs?</h3>
<p>Yes. A creator can replace custom badge images. The member's accumulated loyalty duration remains the same, while the artwork associated with that duration changes.</p>
<p>Frequent redesigns can confuse members, so announce major changes and preserve recognizable elements.</p>

<h3>Do Badges Affect YouTube Recommendations?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not describe loyalty badges as a direct discovery or recommendation-ranking signal. They are a membership recognition and community feature.</p>
<p>Badges may indirectly encourage participation and community identity, but creators should not promise that members' comments will rank higher because of the badge.</p>

<h3>How to Name and Explain the Badge Progression</h3>
<p>YouTube displays the image and loyalty duration, but creators can build additional community meaning around the progression.</p>
<p>Examples:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Seed, Sprout, Tree</li>
  <li>Rookie, Starter, Veteran</li>
  <li>Bronze, Silver, Gold</li>
  <li>Camera, Studio, Production House</li>
</ul>
<p>Keep the theme welcoming. A new member should not feel unimportant simply because the first badge is less elaborate.</p>

<h3>Common Loyalty-Badge Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Designing at full size without testing the rendered size.</li>
  <li>Using text that becomes unreadable.</li>
  <li>Confusing loyalty duration with membership level.</li>
  <li>Making each badge look unrelated.</li>
  <li>Assuming cancellation immediately removes paid-period access.</li>
  <li>Claiming the badge proves channel verification.</li>
  <li>Leaving too much transparent padding.</li>
  <li>Changing the design constantly without telling members.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>What does a YouTube loyalty badge show?</h4>
<p>It shows how long the viewer has been an active paid member of that channel.</p>

<h4>Do higher membership levels receive different badges?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says all members use the same loyalty-based badge progression.</p>

<h4>Where does the badge appear?</h4>
<p>It can appear beside the member's name in comments, live chat, and the channel's Posts tab.</p>

<h4>How small is the badge?</h4>
<p>YouTube currently renders badges at 14 × 14 pixels in comments and Posts and 16 × 16 pixels in live chat.</p>
