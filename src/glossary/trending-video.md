<h2>Trending Video</h2>
<p>A <strong>trending video</strong> is a video experiencing unusually strong or rapidly growing attention relative to its topic, audience, channel, or current cultural moment.</p>
<p>Creators also use the phrase for videos appearing on YouTube's category-specific Trending Charts. These meanings should be separated: a video can be trending within a niche without appearing on an official Chart.</p>

<h3>Trending Is Not a Visibility Setting</h3>
<p>A creator cannot select Trending when uploading a video. YouTube does not provide a universal badge that certifies every fast-growing upload as trending.</p>
<p>Trending can describe rapid view growth, strong current Search activity, a topic spreading across creators, high conversation, official Chart placement, or performance far above a channel's normal range.</p>

<h3>YouTube Trending Charts</h3>
<p>YouTube's current Help documentation describes non-personalized, category-specific Trending Charts for selected areas such as movie trailers, music videos, and gaming.</p>
<p>The Charts:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Show the same ranked videos to viewers in the same country</li>
  <li>Are updated roughly every 30 minutes</li>
  <li>Contain only a limited number of videos</li>
  <li>Require the video to be Public</li>
  <li>Do not accept payment for placement</li>
</ul>

<h3>What YouTube Considers for Trending Charts</h3>
<p>YouTube says the system aims to surface videos that are relevant to the Chart topic, growing quickly, not misleading or sensational, and representative of a diversity of creators.</p>
<p>Signals can include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>View count</li>
  <li>How quickly views are growing</li>
  <li>Where views come from, including outside YouTube</li>
  <li>The topic</li>
  <li>The video's age</li>
  <li>Performance compared with the channel's recent uploads</li>
</ul>

<h3>Highest Views Does Not Automatically Rank First</h3>
<p>YouTube combines several Chart considerations. A video with fewer views can rank above one with more views. A Chart is not simply a sorted view-count leaderboard.</p>

<h3>Advertising Views and Charts</h3>
<p>YouTube says views generated through YouTube ads are not included when measuring videos for Trending Charts. Paid promotion cannot purchase official Chart placement.</p>

<h3>Trending Charts vs. Trends Tab</h3>

  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Purpose</th><th>Personalization</th></tr></thead>

  </tbody>
</table>
<p>A video shown as a Breakout Video in Studio is not necessarily on an official public Trending Chart.</p>

<h3>Trending Video vs. Breakout Video</h3>
<p><a href="/glossary/breakout-videos/">Breakout Videos</a> highlights well-performing content from creators whose channels are similar to the creator using Studio.</p>
<p>It is a personalized inspiration report, not a universal declaration that the video is trending throughout YouTube.</p>

<h3>Trending Video vs. Topical Content</h3>
<p>Topical content covers a current subject. A trending video is receiving rapidly growing attention. A creator can publish a topical video too early, too late, or with too little audience interest for it to trend.</p>

<h3>Trending Video vs. Viral Video</h3>
<p>The terms overlap but have no single YouTube-required definition.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Trending:</strong> Attention is currently rising or prominent.</li>
  <li><strong>Viral:</strong> The content spreads unusually widely and rapidly, often through sharing and network effects.</li>
</ul>

<h3>How to Find Trends in YouTube Studio</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Analytics</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open the <strong>Trends</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Review Top Searches, Breakout Videos, Recent Videos, and content gaps.</li>
  <li>Search a topic and review Audience Interest, Watched on YouTube, and Searched on YouTube.</li>
  <li>Save useful searches.</li>
</ol>
<p>Starting in July 2026, YouTube began gradually rolling out an updated Studio Trends experience, so available sections may differ by account, device, language, and country.</p>

<h3>Top Searches</h3>
<p>The current Trends Tab Top Searches card uses the creator's audience and saved searches over the last 28 days. It is useful for current demand, but it is not a live ranking of every query on YouTube.</p>

<h3>Audience Interest</h3>
<p>Current <a href="/glossary/audience-interest/">Audience Interest</a> levels range from very low to very high for eligible ideas. This is a directional signal, not a forecast of how many views the creator's video will receive.</p>

<h3>Watched on YouTube</h3>
<p>This section shows popular videos watched across YouTube for a searched topic. Study topic angles, viewer expectations, video age, formats, unanswered questions, and whether the interest appears broad or niche.</p>

<h3>Trending Does Not Mean Copying</h3>
<p>Copying a trending title, thumbnail, script, sound, or visual can create copyright concerns, reused-content concerns, weak differentiation, audience confusion, and late entry after saturation.</p>
<p>Use the trend as context and add an original angle that serves the channel's audience.</p>

<h3>Make the Trend Fit the Channel</h3>
<p>YouTube's creator guidance recommends adapting trends to the creator's existing subject and loyal audience.</p>
<ul>
  <li>A makeup channel interprets a movie release through special-effects makeup.</li>
  <li>A camera channel tests equipment used in a viral filming style.</li>
  <li>A finance channel explains the economic issue behind a current story.</li>
  <li>A gaming channel analyzes the mechanic driving a popular trend.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Trend Lifecycles</h3>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Emergence:</strong> A small group begins creating or searching.</li>
  <li><strong>Acceleration:</strong> View and search velocity rises.</li>
  <li><strong>Mainstream:</strong> Many creators and viewers recognize the trend.</li>
  <li><strong>Saturation:</strong> Similar content becomes abundant.</li>
  <li><strong>Decline:</strong> Attention falls or moves elsewhere.</li>
  <li><strong>Legacy:</strong> The trend becomes a recurring format, niche, or reference.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Should You Enter Early?</h3>
<p>Early entry offers lower competition but less certainty. Late entry offers clearer demand but more competition.</p>
<p>Enter when the topic fits the audience, the creator has a distinct contribution, facts and rights can be verified, production can finish in time, and the video remains useful even if it does not trend.</p>

<h3>Trend Chasing Risks</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Attracting viewers who do not care about the channel</li>
  <li>Confusing regular viewers</li>
  <li>Publishing after saturation</li>
  <li>Weak authority</li>
  <li>Burnout from constant urgency</li>
  <li>Copyright or misinformation errors</li>
  <li>Building no durable library</li>
</ul>

<h3>New Viewers and Trending Videos</h3>
<p>YouTube says a high share of <a href="/glossary/new-viewers/">New Viewers</a> is common for trending videos. Those viewers may care only about the specific topic.</p>
<p>Evaluate whether the trend also creates subscribers, casual viewers, regular viewers, related-video views, or business value.</p>

<h3>Packaging a Trend Video</h3>
<p>The packaging should identify the trend or event, the creator's distinct angle, the viewer benefit, and the correct level of urgency.</p>
<p>Avoid pretending an old trend is still breaking or using names and images unrelated to the actual video.</p>

<h3>Trend Audio and Shorts</h3>
<p>Trending audio can help a Short participate in a familiar creative pattern, but audio alone does not guarantee distribution.</p>
<p>Use eligible audio and remix tools, add original value, and ensure the visual idea works for viewers unfamiliar with the sound.</p>

<h3>External Sources of Trend Data</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Google Trends</li>
  <li>Industry publications</li>
  <li>Search suggestions</li>
  <li>Social platforms</li>
  <li>Reddit and online communities</li>
  <li>News and entertainment schedules</li>
  <li>Customer conversations</li>
</ul>
<p>Verify that a cross-platform trend is relevant to YouTube and the channel's audience.</p>

<h3>Official Charts Are Category-Limited</h3>
<p>Do not assume every content category has the same public Chart. Current Help identifies selected categories such as movie trailers, music videos, and gaming.</p>
<p>A creator can have a strongly trending video without an available public Chart for the topic.</p>

<h3>Chart Safety and Eligibility</h3>
<p>YouTube says Trending Charts use safety filters and avoid videos with excessive profanity, mature content, violence, or other inappropriate material. A video must be Public to appear.</p>

<h3>Trending Is Not Permanent Success</h3>
<p>A trend spike can produce high views but low return behavior, subscribers who ignore later uploads, strong revenue during a short window, heavy external traffic, or pressure to repeat an unrelated topic.</p>

<h3>Turn a Trend Into a Durable Asset</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Publish the timely angle.</li>
  <li>Identify the recurring question behind it.</li>
  <li>Create a deeper lasting guide.</li>
  <li>Organize related videos in a playlist.</li>
  <li>Use end screens and related links.</li>
  <li>Update or retire the topical video when necessary.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Measuring a Trending Video</h3>
<ul>
  <li>View velocity</li>
  <li>Traffic-source mix</li>
  <li>External sharing</li>
  <li>Search activity</li>
  <li>Viewer choice and retention</li>
  <li>New vs. returning viewers</li>
  <li>Subscriber conversion</li>
  <li>Views on related content</li>
  <li>Performance after the trend declines</li>
</ul>

<h3>Common Trending-Video Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Calling every high-view video officially trending.</li>
  <li>Confusing Trends Tab, Breakout Videos, and public Charts.</li>
  <li>Assuming the highest view count ranks first.</li>
  <li>Buying ads and expecting Chart placement.</li>
  <li>Copying another creator's expression.</li>
  <li>Entering a trend unrelated to the channel.</li>
  <li>Ignoring the likely decline in demand.</li>
  <li>Judging success only by the initial spike.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>Can I mark my upload as Trending?</h4>
<p>No. Trending is not an upload visibility setting.</p>

<h4>Are YouTube Trending Charts personalized?</h4>
<p>No. Current category Charts show the same ranked videos to viewers in the same country.</p>

<h4>Does the video with the most views rank first?</h4>
<p>Not necessarily. YouTube considers growth, traffic sources, topic, age, channel-relative performance, and other factors.</p>

<h4>Can ads buy a place on Trending Charts?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says ad views are excluded from Chart measurement and payment is not accepted for placement.</p>
