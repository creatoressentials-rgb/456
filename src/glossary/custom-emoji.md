<h2>Custom Emoji</h2>
<p><strong>YouTube custom emoji</strong> are channel-specific images that active channel members can use in comments on the creator's videos and in live chats.</p>
<p>Creators upload and name the emoji through the Memberships section of YouTube Studio. All active members receive access to the same custom emoji library regardless of membership level.</p>

<h3>Where Can Members Use Custom Emoji?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Comments on the channel's videos</li>
  <li>Comments on eligible Shorts</li>
  <li>Live chat on the channel's live streams</li>
  <li>Live chat during Premieres</li>
  <li>Other eligible channel comment and chat surfaces</li>
</ul>
<p>Custom emoji are a channel-membership perk. Viewers without an active membership can see the emoji when members use them but cannot select them for their own messages.</p>

<h3>Do Different Membership Levels Get Different Emoji?</h3>
<p>No. YouTube says all members, regardless of level, get access to every custom emoji the creator uploads.</p>
<p>A creator cannot reserve one standard custom emoji for only the highest-priced level through YouTube's normal membership-emoji controls.</p>

<h3>How to Upload Custom Emoji</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Earn</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Memberships</strong>.</li>
  <li>Find the badges and emoji card.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Edit</strong>.</li>
  <li>Create or confirm the emoji family name.</li>
  <li>Upload each image.</li>
  <li>Assign an emoji name.</li>
  <li>Preview and save the changes.</li>
</ol>

<h3>What Is the Emoji Family Name?</h3>
<p>The family name is a shared prefix for the channel's emoji. Members use it to find and autocomplete the emoji in live chat.</p>
<p>Current YouTube rules include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>It must contain 3–10 characters.</li>
  <li>It does not need to be unique across YouTube.</li>
  <li>YouTube recommends a recognizable channel or brand-related name.</li>
  <li>Members can also begin autocomplete with <code>:_</code>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>What Is an Emoji Name?</h3>
<p>Each uploaded emoji has its own name inside the family.</p>
<p>Current rules include:</p>
<ul>
  <li>3–10 alphanumeric characters</li>
  <li>Unique within the channel's emoji family</li>
  <li>Not required to be unique across all of YouTube</li>
</ul>
<p>To change an existing emoji name, YouTube says the creator must delete and re-upload that emoji.</p>

<h3>Example Emoji Naming System</h3>
<p>A channel with the family name <code>create</code> might use:</p>
<ul>
  <li><code>:create_hype:</code></li>
  <li><code>:create_lol:</code></li>
  <li><code>:create_win:</code></li>
  <li><code>:create_idea:</code></li>
</ul>
<p>Short, memorable names are easier to type and autocomplete during a fast live chat.</p>

<h3>Custom Emoji vs. Standard Unicode Emoji</h3>
<p>Standard emoji are part of the viewer's keyboard and can be used widely. YouTube custom emoji are uploaded by one creator and tied to that channel's membership program.</p>
<p>A custom emoji can contain a mascot, recurring joke, branded symbol, reaction face, or channel-specific visual that standard emoji cannot express.</p>

<h3>How to Design Readable Emoji</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Use one clear emotion or idea.</li>
  <li>Crop tightly around the subject.</li>
  <li>Use strong contrast.</li>
  <li>Avoid long words.</li>
  <li>Use thick outlines when they improve separation.</li>
  <li>Remove unnecessary background detail.</li>
  <li>Preview at 24 × 24 points.</li>
  <li>Test on light and dark interfaces.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Good Custom Emoji Categories</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Celebration</li>
  <li>Laughter</li>
  <li>Surprise</li>
  <li>Agreement</li>
  <li>Confusion</li>
  <li>Channel catchphrases</li>
  <li>Recurring characters or mascots</li>
  <li>Topic-specific reactions</li>
  <li>Live-stream commands or rituals</li>
</ul>
<p>Start with reactions members will use repeatedly rather than obscure references that only work once.</p>

<h3>Emoji for Live Chat</h3>
<p>Live chat moves quickly, so the strongest emoji have:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Short names</li>
  <li>Distinct silhouettes</li>
  <li>Immediate emotional meaning</li>
  <li>Minimal fine detail</li>
  <li>Clear differences from the other channel emoji</li>
</ul>

<h3>Emoji for Comments</h3>
<p>In video comments, members can use emoji to show support, react to a moment, or participate in a recurring community joke.</p>
<p>Creators should avoid designing an emoji that only makes sense in one live stream when the same library is intended for comments across the channel.</p>

<h3>Can GIF Emoji Animate?</h3>
<p>No. YouTube currently accepts GIF files as a preferred upload format but displays them as static images.</p>
<p>Creators should select a strong single frame rather than relying on motion to communicate the expression.</p>

<h3>Transparent Backgrounds</h3>
<p>PNG files are often useful for transparent backgrounds. Transparency can help the emoji integrate with different interface colors.</p>
<p>Keep enough outline and contrast so a transparent emoji does not disappear on dark or light backgrounds.</p>

<h3>Replacing or Renaming Emoji</h3>
<p>A creator can replace an image, but changing the emoji name requires deletion and re-upload according to YouTube's current guidance.</p>
<p>Before removing a commonly used emoji:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Tell members about the change.</li>
  <li>Preserve familiar naming where practical.</li>
  <li>Avoid replacing the entire library at once.</li>
  <li>Keep source files and naming records.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Do Custom Emoji Affect Recommendations?</h3>
<p>YouTube does not describe custom emoji as a direct recommendation-ranking signal. They are a membership and community-expression perk.</p>
<p>They can encourage participation and strengthen community identity, but creators should not promise algorithmic reach from emoji use.</p>

<h3>Copyright and Trademark Rules</h3>
<p>Creators should upload artwork they own or have permission to use. A custom emoji should not copy another creator's mascot, copyrighted character, company logo, or protected artwork without authorization.</p>
<p>The small display size does not eliminate intellectual-property obligations.</p>

<h3>How to Choose the First Four Emoji</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Choose the most common positive reaction.</li>
  <li>Add a funny or surprised reaction.</li>
  <li>Add one channel-specific mascot or phrase.</li>
  <li>Add one topic-specific reaction members will use often.</li>
  <li>Observe usage before filling newly unlocked slots.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Common Custom-Emoji Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Designing for a large canvas instead of 24-point display.</li>
  <li>Assuming GIFs animate.</li>
  <li>Creating long or confusing emoji names.</li>
  <li>Trying to reserve emoji for one membership tier.</li>
  <li>Waiting for an unlock notification that YouTube does not send.</li>
  <li>Using artwork without permission.</li>
  <li>Uploading too many similar reactions.</li>
  <li>Changing popular names without warning members.</li>
</ul>
