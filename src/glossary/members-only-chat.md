<h2>Members-Only Chat</h2>
<p><strong>Members-only chat</strong> is a YouTube live-chat setting that allows only active channel members to send messages while a public live stream remains viewable by everyone.</p>
<p>It restricts participation in chat—not access to the video. This makes it different from a members-only live stream.</p>

<h3>Who Can Watch and Who Can Chat?</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Viewer</th><th>Can Watch Public Stream?</th><th>Can Send Chat Messages?</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Qualifying channel member</td><td>Yes</td><td>Yes</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Subscriber who is not a member</td><td>Yes</td><td>No</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Non-subscriber</td><td>Yes</td><td>No</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Creator or approved moderator</td><td>Yes</td><td>According to YouTube's moderation permissions</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<p>All viewers can generally read the messages shown in the public stream's chat.</p>

<h3>How to Enable Members-Only Chat</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Open YouTube Studio on a computer.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Create</strong> and then <strong>Go live</strong>.</li>
  <li>Create or reuse a stream.</li>
  <li>Open the stream's Edit settings.</li>
  <li>Open Live chat.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Enable members-only chat</strong>.</li>
  <li>Save the stream settings.</li>
</ol>
<p>The exact control location can change with Live Control Room updates.</p>

<h3>Members-Only Chat vs. Members-Only Live Stream</h3>
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Feature</th><th>Members-Only Chat</th><th>Members-Only Live Stream</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Video access</td><td>Public viewers can watch</td><td>Only qualifying members can watch</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Chat access</td><td>Members can post</td><td>Viewers with access can participate subject to settings</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Discovery value</td><td>Broad public reach</td><td>Exclusive member experience</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Primary perk</td><td>Recognition and participation</td><td>Exclusive content access</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<h3>Members-Only Chat vs. Subscribers-Only Chat</h3>
<p>Subscribers-only chat lets viewers send messages after subscribing for a creator-selected amount of time. Members-only chat requires a paid channel membership.</p>
<p>YouTube says members-only chat and subscribers-only chat cannot be enabled at the same time.</p>

<h3>Members-Only Chat vs. Live Commentary Mode</h3>
<p>Live Commentary Mode limits posting to approved users selected by the creator. Members-only chat allows the broader group of active channel members.</p>
<p>Use Live Commentary for controlled panels or official commentary; use members-only chat to reward the membership community.</p>

<h3>Does Slow Mode Apply to Members?</h3>
<p>YouTube's current membership-benefits guidance says Slow Mode does not apply to active paid channel members.</p>
<p>Creators should still use moderation tools, chat guidelines, and trained moderators when a large member community is active.</p>

<h3>Can a Membership Level Be Required?</h3>
<p>YouTube's public members-only chat instructions describe enabling chat for channel members generally. The documented stream-visibility controls support level-based viewing, but creators should not assume every chat interface offers a separate minimum-tier selector.</p>
<p>When tier-specific access is essential, a level-restricted members-only live stream is the clearer control.</p>

<h3>Can It Be Used on a Premiere?</h3>
<p>YouTube live-chat settings and feature availability can differ between live streams and Premieres. Current viewer membership guidance explicitly describes members-only chat during public live streams.</p>
<p>Creators should verify the Participants Mode options shown for the specific Premiere rather than assuming every live-chat setting is identical.</p>

<h3>What Viewers See</h3>
<p>Non-members can watch the public stream and read chat, but the message field indicates that participation is restricted.</p>
<p>This can demonstrate a membership perk without placing the content itself behind a paywall.</p>

<h3>Why Use Members-Only Chat?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Give members more visibility in a busy public stream</li>
  <li>Reduce the volume of messages</li>
  <li>Create a closer discussion</li>
  <li>Reward recurring supporters</li>
  <li>Make Q&amp;A questions easier to manage</li>
  <li>Show non-members a concrete membership benefit</li>
</ul>

<h3>Potential Downsides</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Non-members may feel excluded from the conversation.</li>
  <li>Public engagement can decrease.</li>
  <li>A small membership base can create a quiet chat.</li>
  <li>Viewers may confuse chat access with video access.</li>
  <li>Members may expect replies to every message.</li>
  <li>Moderation is still required.</li>
</ul>

<h3>When to Turn It On</h3>
<p>Members-only chat can work well for:</p>
<ul>
  <li>A dedicated member question segment</li>
  <li>A portion of a long public broadcast</li>
  <li>A product or project debrief</li>
  <li>A high-volume stream where member questions need priority</li>
  <li>A recurring membership perk at a predictable time</li>
</ul>
<p>It does not need to remain enabled for every public stream.</p>

<h3>How to Explain the Setting</h3>
<p>Use a direct explanation, such as:</p>
<ul>
  <li>"Everyone can watch, but chat is open to channel members for this segment."</li>
  <li>"Member questions are prioritized during the final 20 minutes."</li>
  <li>"Join at any channel-membership level to participate in today's chat."</li>
</ul>
<p>Avoid implying that viewers must pay to watch when only chat is restricted.</p>

<h3>Moderation Tools Still Apply</h3>
<p>Creators can continue using:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Moderators</li>
  <li>Blocked words</li>
  <li>Held potentially inappropriate messages</li>
  <li>User timeouts</li>
  <li>Hidden users</li>
  <li>Chat deletion</li>
  <li>Live-chat guidelines</li>
</ul>
<p>Paying for membership does not exempt a viewer from Community Guidelines or channel moderation.</p>

<h3>Member Badges and Emoji</h3>
<p>Members can display loyalty badges and use channel emoji in live chat when the creator has enabled those perks.</p>
<p>These visual features can help the creator recognize tenure and participation, but they should not replace clear moderation decisions.</p>

<h3>New Member Messages</h3>
<p>When a viewer joins during a live stream, YouTube can display a bright green new-member message and pin the member's profile image at the top of chat for five minutes.</p>
<p>This is a separate membership recognition feature, not the same as turning the entire chat members-only.</p>

<h3>Member Milestone Chats</h3>
<p>Beginning with a member's second consecutive month, YouTube provides a monthly Member Milestone Chat that highlights the viewer's membership duration.</p>
<p>The message can appear publicly in live chat even when the stream itself is public.</p>

<h3>Members-Only Chat and Super Chat</h3>
<p><a href="/glossary/super-chat/">Super Chat</a> is a paid highlighted-message feature governed by separate eligibility and transaction rules. Members-only chat controls who may participate.</p>
<p>A channel member is not guaranteed free Super Chats, and enabling member chat does not automatically enable fan-funding purchases.</p>

<h3>Analytics and Evaluation</h3>
<p>YouTube does not provide one simple "members-only chat success score." Evaluate:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Active chat participants</li>
  <li>Message quality</li>
  <li>Membership joins and cancellations</li>
  <li>Concurrent viewers</li>
  <li>Watch time</li>
  <li>Moderator workload</li>
  <li>Member feedback</li>
  <li>Public-viewer response</li>
</ul>

<h3>Members-Only Chat as a Sustainable Perk</h3>
<p>Chat access is often less production-intensive than creating a separate exclusive video every week. It can be a useful lightweight perk when the creator already streams publicly.</p>
<p>Set clear expectations about:</p>
<ul>
  <li>How often it will be used</li>
  <li>Whether every question will be answered</li>
  <li>Which streams include it</li>
  <li>How moderation works</li>
</ul>

<h3>Common Members-Only-Chat Mistakes</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Assuming only members can watch the stream.</li>
  <li>Combining it with subscribers-only chat.</li>
  <li>Promising tier-specific chat without confirming the control.</li>
  <li>Failing to explain the restriction to public viewers.</li>
  <li>Assuming paying members cannot be moderated.</li>
  <li>Confusing member messages with Super Chats.</li>
  <li>Expecting the setting to create an active community automatically.</li>
  <li>Using it on every stream without considering public engagement.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
<h4>Can non-members watch a members-only chat stream?</h4>
<p>Yes. The stream can remain public while only members are allowed to post messages.</p>

<h4>Can subscribers chat without paying?</h4>
<p>Not when members-only chat is enabled. Subscription alone does not provide channel-membership access.</p>

<h4>Can subscribers-only and members-only chat run together?</h4>
<p>No. YouTube says the two participant modes cannot be enabled at the same time.</p>

<h4>Can members still be moderated?</h4>
<p>Yes. Membership does not exempt a viewer from chat rules or moderation.</p>
