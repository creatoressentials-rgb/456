<h2>YouTube Video Description</h2>


<h2>What is a YouTube Video Description?</h2>

<p>A YouTube description provides insights into either a <a href="/glossary/youtube-channel/">YouTube channel</a> or a specific video. There are two types: channel descriptions and video descriptions. A channel description details what the channel is about, including its genre, what viewers can expect, the video publishing schedule, social media and website links, and any important news.</p>
<p>A video description, found below each video, gives a brief overview of the video's content, relevant <a href="/glossary/keywords/">keywords</a>, links to resources mentioned in the video, affiliate links, and a prompt for viewers to subscribe.</p>

<h2>Why Are YouTube Video Descriptions Important?</h2>

<p>YouTube descriptions are critical because they help both YouTube's algorithm and viewers understand the content and context of a video. Well-optimized descriptions can significantly boost a video's search rankings and visibility on the platform</p>

<h2>What’s the Difference Between a YouTube Channel Description and a Video Description?</h2>

<p>The main difference lies in their focus and content.</p>
<p>Channel descriptions are about the overall channel, its theme, and what viewers can expect. They provide a general overview of the channel's content and its unique appeal.</p>

<h3>YouTube Video Descriptions</h3>

<p>In contrast, video descriptions are specific to each video, detailing what the video is about. They include relevant links and prompts, offering viewers additional context and resources related to the video's content.</p>

<h2>What Should Be Included in a YouTube Video Description?</h2>

<p>Video chapters should include the following:​</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Keywords and <a href="/glossary/seo/">SEO</a>: Include 1-2 relevant keywords, repeated 2-3 times, to aid in search visibility. The first 2-3 sentences should focus on these keywords as they are most visible in search results and impact click-through rates​​​​​​​.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Length and Content: Aim for over 200 words, ensuring the description is thorough and informative. This helps YouTube's algorithm understand and rank the video better​​.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Related Keywords: Include related terms to give additional context and improve ranking for those terms in searches​.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Originality: Each video should have a unique description to enhance findability and distinctiveness​​.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Video Chapters: Including chapters improves the user experience and allows search engines to understand different parts of the video.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><a href="/glossary/hashtags/">Hashtags</a>: YouTube rewards videos that include hashtags and even highlight the first three hashtags found in the video description.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Links to Other Videos: Link to similar videos to encourage users to explore additional content on your channel.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Links to Your Website: Encourage users to visit your website buy products and more!</p>
</li>
</ul>
