VidIQ's coaching program is well-known, but it's far from your only option. If you're looking for [personalized YouTube coaching](/services/youtube-coaching-program/), here's a clear breakdown of what VidIQ offers — and seven alternatives worth considering.

## VidIQ's YouTube Coaching Program

VidIQ's coaching targets creators who want structured, data-driven guidance with emphasis on channel analytics, [SEO](/glossary/seo/), and content strategy.

**What you get:**
- Dedicated 1-on-1 Creator Coach for audits and strategy
- Channel analysis to identify improvement areas
- SEO-focused content planning (titles, [thumbnails](/glossary/youtube-thumbnails/), [keywords](/glossary/keywords/))

**Price:** $199/month ($99/month paid annually)

For most creators looking for structured, data-driven coaching, vidIQ's program is worth serious consideration before exploring other options.

[Learn more about vidIQ's coaching program](https://vidiq.com/creatoressential)

## 6 Alternatives to VidIQ Coaching

### 1. LaShonda Brown

**Price:** $500 for a 60-minute session / $50/month membership  
**Specializes in:** SEO-driven growth and lead generation for service-based businesses

### 2. Christine Kaaloa

**Price:** $250 for a 60-minute session  
**Specializes in:** Travel [vlogging](/glossary/vlog/) and [niche](/glossary/niche/) content strategy, plus mindset coaching for creators overcoming fear of camera

### 3. Creator Coach

**Price:** Custom pricing for 45-minute sessions  
**Specializes in:** Work-life balance, stress management, and sustainable long-term creator success. Works with channels ranging from new to 500K+ [subscribers](/glossary/youtube-subscribers/)

### 4. Acceleratus Media (Owen Video)

**Price:** Custom pricing  
**Specializes in:** YouTube for businesses and executives. Lead-generation strategies, VA training, and done-for-you channel optimization for brands using YouTube as a primary marketing channel

### 5. Erika Vieira

**Price:** 5-figure investment  
**Specializes in:** Women YouTubers targeting growth from 100K to 1M+ subscribers. Deep-dive branding, audience psychology, and daily channel monitoring

### 6. Creator People

**Price:** $1,100 for 3–4 sessions (1.5 hours each)  
**Specializes in:** Multi-session structured coaching with in-depth analytics and SEO training

## Which Is Right for You?

The right coaching program depends on your goals, budget, and where your channel currently stands.

If you want a structured program backed by a major YouTube analytics platform — [vidIQ's coaching program](https://vidiq.com/creatoressential) is the natural starting point.

If you're a business owner using YouTube for leads, Owen Video's focus on ROI and conversion may be a better fit.

If you're a solo creator ready for a premium, high-investment engagement, Erika Vieira works at a different scale.

The most important thing: don't keep guessing on your own when expert guidance is available at multiple price points.
